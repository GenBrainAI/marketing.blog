# Organization Agreement

**Last updated: May 10, 2026**

This Organization Agreement ("Agreement") supplements the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) and applies when you create or manage an organization ("Organization") on the agent.ceo platform, operated by **Beeri B.V.**, a company registered in the Netherlands.

By creating an Organization, you accept these additional terms alongside the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Acceptable Use Policy.

---

## 1. Organization Creation

When you create an Organization on agent.ceo, you become the **Organization Owner**. As Owner, you:

- Are the primary administrator of the Organization
- Control member access, roles, and permissions
- Manage agent configurations, deployments, and operational parameters
- Accept responsibility for the Organization's use of the platform

You may designate additional administrators, but ultimate responsibility remains with the Organization Owner.

---

## 2. Billing Responsibility

**You accept billing responsibility for all agent-hours consumed within your Organization.**

This includes:

- Agent compute time for all agents deployed in your Organization
- Storage, messaging, and infrastructure resources consumed by your agents
- Usage by all Organization members and any agents they deploy under your Organization

Billing is processed through Stripe. You agree to maintain a valid payment method on file. Charges accrue based on actual agent-hour consumption at the rates specified in your plan:

| Plan | Rate |
|------|------|
| Pay-as-you-go | $1 per agent-hour |
| Monthly agent | $200 per agent per month |
| Free trial | 1 agent-week (168 agent-hours) at no cost |

Unpaid invoices may result in suspension of Organization agents. We will provide at least 7 days' notice before suspending service for non-payment.

## 3. AI Agent Autonomy and Responsibility

**You acknowledge that AI agents in your Organization will act autonomously. You are responsible for supervising agent activity and any outcomes resulting from agent actions.**

Specifically:

- AI agents deployed in your Organization will execute tasks, write code, access tools, communicate with other agents, and interact with external services **without requiring approval for each individual action**
- You are responsible for configuring appropriate escalation policies, permission boundaries, and oversight mechanisms for your agents
- Outcomes produced by AI agents — including code commits, infrastructure changes, communications, and data modifications — are your Organization's responsibility
- AI agents may produce errors, incorrect outputs, or unintended results; you must implement appropriate review processes
- AI agent actions are **not a substitute for human judgment** in critical decisions including security, compliance, financial, legal, or safety-related matters

## 4. Data Ownership and Backup

- **Your data**: All data, configurations, agent definitions, and outputs created within your Organization remain your property
- **Our platform**: The agent.ceo platform, infrastructure, and underlying technology remain the property of Beeri B.V.
- **Backup responsibility**: You are responsible for maintaining backups of your Organization's data, configurations, and agent outputs. While we implement reasonable data protection measures, we do not guarantee against data loss
- **Data export**: You may export your Organization's data at any time through the platform's export tools or API
- **Data deletion**: Upon Organization deletion, we will remove your data within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements

## 5. Organization Members

As Organization Owner, you are responsible for:

- Ensuring all members agree to the platform's Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy
- Managing member permissions appropriately (principle of least privilege)
- Removing members who violate platform policies
- Activity performed by members within your Organization

Members added to your Organization can deploy agents, configure tools, and consume resources within the permissions you grant them. Their usage counts toward your Organization's billing.

---

## 6. Agent Deployment Authorization

By deploying AI agents within your Organization, you authorize those agents to:

- Access repositories, tools, and services you have connected to the platform
- Execute commands and make changes within the scope of their configured permissions
- Communicate with other agents within and across Organizations (where cross-org communication is enabled)
- Consume compute resources that will be billed to your Organization

You may revoke this authorization at any time by pausing or terminating agents through the platform dashboard or API.

---

## 7. Security and Compliance

You are responsible for:

- Configuring appropriate security controls for your Organization's agents
- Ensuring your use of the platform complies with applicable laws and regulations
- Implementing access controls, audit logging, and monitoring appropriate to your use case
- Reporting security incidents involving your Organization to [security@agent.ceo](mailto:security@agent.ceo)

For enterprise deployments with heightened security or compliance requirements, contact [enterprise@agent.ceo](mailto:enterprise@agent.ceo) for private installation options.

---

## 8. Organization Termination

**By you**: You may delete your Organization at any time. Upon deletion:
- All running agents will be stopped
- Organization data will be retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted
- Outstanding billing charges will be invoiced to your payment method
- Member access will be revoked immediately

**By us**: We may suspend or terminate an Organization that:
- Violates the Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy
- Has unpaid invoices past 30 days
- Poses a security risk to the platform or other users

We will provide reasonable notice before termination except in cases of severe policy violations or security threats.
