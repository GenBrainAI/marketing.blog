# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: May 10, 2026**

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of the agent.ceo platform ("Service"), operated by **Beeri B.V.** ("Company", "we", "us", "our"). This policy is incorporated into and forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service).

By using the Service, you agree to comply with this policy. Violations may result in warnings, suspension, or termination of your account.

---

## 1. Your Responsibility for AI Agent Actions

You are **fully responsible** for all actions taken by AI agents operating within your account. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Content generated or published by your agents.
- Communications sent by your agents to internal or external recipients.
- Data accessed, processed, or modified by your agents.
- Compute resources consumed by your agents.
- Any downstream consequences of agent actions, whether intended or unintended.

Deploying AI agents does not transfer or reduce your accountability. "My agent did it" is not a defense for policy violations.

## 2. Prohibited Uses

You may **not** use the Service for any of the following:

### 2.1 Illegal Activities

- Conducting, facilitating, or promoting any activity that violates applicable local, national, or international law.
- Money laundering, fraud, identity theft, or other financial crimes.
- Distribution of illegal content or materials.

### 2.2 Harassment and Abuse

- Harassing, threatening, intimidating, or stalking any individual.
- Generating or distributing content that promotes hate, discrimination, or violence against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, or other protected characteristics.
- Impersonating individuals, organizations, or government entities.

### 2.3 Spam and Unsolicited Communications

- Sending unsolicited bulk messages, emails, or communications.
- Using agents for automated spam, phishing, or social engineering attacks.
- Scraping or harvesting personal data for unsolicited outreach.

### 2.4 Malware and Security Threats

- Creating, distributing, or deploying malware, ransomware, viruses, or other malicious software.
- Conducting denial-of-service attacks or other attacks against any system.
- Exploiting vulnerabilities in any system, whether ours or third-party.

### 2.5 Harmful Content Generation

- Generating content that is illegal, defamatory, obscene, or harmful.
- Creating deepfakes, misleading synthetic media, or disinformation intended to deceive.
- Producing content that exploits or endangers minors.

## 3. Security and Platform Integrity

You may **not**:

- Attempt to circumvent, disable, or interfere with any security controls, authentication mechanisms, or rate limits of the Service.
- Access accounts, data, or systems that do not belong to you.
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service without prior written authorization.
- Interfere with or disrupt the integrity or performance of the Service for other users.
- Reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the source code or underlying algorithms of the platform.
- Create derivative works based on the Service without prior written consent.

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to **security@agent.ceo**. We welcome responsible disclosure and will not take legal action against good-faith security researchers.

## 4. Resource Limits and Fair Use

The Service operates on a shared infrastructure. To ensure fair access for all users:

- **Compute limits:** Use agent compute resources in accordance with your plan and in a manner consistent with normal platform usage patterns.
- **No cryptocurrency mining:** Using agent compute for cryptocurrency mining, proof-of-work calculations, or similar resource-intensive computations unrelated to the Service is prohibited.
- **No excessive resource abuse:** Do not intentionally consume disproportionate compute, storage, or network resources in a manner that degrades the Service for others.
- **Rate limits:** Respect API and platform rate limits. Do not attempt to circumvent or bypass them through any means.

We reserve the right to throttle, suspend, or limit accounts that consume resources in a manner we determine to be abusive or disproportionate.

## 6. Content Standards

Content generated, stored, or distributed through the Service must not:

- Be illegal or promote illegal activities.
- Be harmful, threatening, abusive, harassing, defamatory, or discriminatory.
- Infringe on the intellectual property rights of any third party.
- Contain personal data of individuals without proper consent or legal basis.
- Violate the privacy or publicity rights of any person.
- Be misleading, deceptive, or fraudulent.

You are responsible for ensuring that content generated by your AI agents meets these standards.

## 7. AI-Specific Provisions

### 7.1 AI Agents May Produce Errors

AI agents are probabilistic systems. They **may and will** produce outputs that are:

- Incorrect or inaccurate.
- Incomplete or missing important context.
- Inconsistent across different runs.
- Confidently wrong (presenting incorrect information with apparent certainty).

### 7.2 User Verification Required

You **must** verify and review agent outputs before relying on them, particularly for:

- Business decisions with financial impact.
- Communications sent to customers, partners, or the public.
- Legal, regulatory, or compliance matters.
- Technical decisions affecting production systems.
- Any situation where errors could cause harm.

### 7.3 Not for Life-Safety Decisions

The Service is **not designed or intended** for use in life-safety applications. Do not use AI agents for:

- Medical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
- Autonomous vehicle control or aviation systems.
- Emergency response coordination where human lives are at stake.
- Critical infrastructure control (power grids, water systems, etc.).
- Any application where an agent error could result in physical injury or death.

## 8. Monitoring and Enforcement

### 8.1 Monitoring

Beeri B.V. **may** monitor platform usage for the purposes of:

- Maintaining security and preventing abuse.
- Ensuring compliance with this policy and applicable law.
- Enforcing resource limits and fair use.
- Investigating reported violations.

We do **not** routinely inspect the content of your agent outputs, configurations, or data. Monitoring is focused on usage patterns, resource consumption, and security signals.

### 8.2 Enforcement Actions

When we identify a violation of this policy, we may take one or more of the following actions, depending on the severity and nature of the violation:

| Severity | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **Minor / First offense** | Written warning via email with description of the violation and required corrective action. |
| **Repeated or moderate** | Temporary suspension of the account or specific agents, with a clear path to reinstatement. |
| **Severe or illegal** | Immediate termination of the account without prior notice. We may also report the activity to relevant authorities. |

We will make reasonable efforts to notify you before or promptly after taking enforcement action, except where doing so would compromise security, enable continued harm, or violate legal requirements.

### 8.3 Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action was taken in error, you may appeal by contacting support@agent.ceo within 30 days of the action. We will review your appeal and respond within 14 business days.

## 9. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of any use of the Service that violates this policy, please report it to:

- **Security issues:** security@agent.ceo
- **General violations:** support@agent.ceo

We take all reports seriously and will investigate promptly. You may report anonymously.
