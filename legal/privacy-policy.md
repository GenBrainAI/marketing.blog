# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 10, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Beeri B.V.** ("Company", "we", "us", "our"), a company registered in the Netherlands, collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use the agent.ceo platform ("Service").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently and responsibly.

---

## 1. Data Controller

The data controller for personal data processed through the Service is:

**Beeri B.V.**
The Netherlands

For privacy-related inquiries: privacy@agent.ceo
For general support: support@agent.ceo

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Organization name (if applicable)
- Password (stored in hashed form only)
- Billing address (for paid plans)

### 2.2 Payment Data

Payment information (credit card numbers, bank details) is collected and processed directly by **Stripe**, our payment processor. We do not store full payment card details on our servers. We receive only a tokenized reference and basic transaction records from Stripe.

### 2.3 Usage Data

We collect data about how you use the Service, including:

- Agent configurations, task definitions, and workflow settings
- Agent execution logs and outputs
- API calls and platform interactions
- Session duration and feature usage patterns
- IP addresses, browser type, and device information

### 2.4 Communication Data

If you contact us via email or support channels, we retain the content of those communications to respond to and resolve your inquiries.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---------|-----------|
| **Providing the Service** | Account info, agent configurations, usage data |
| **Billing and payments** | Account info, payment data (via Stripe) |
| **Service improvement** | Aggregated and anonymized usage data |
| **Security and fraud prevention** | IP addresses, usage patterns, access logs |
| **Customer support** | Account info, communication data |
| **Legal compliance** | As required by applicable law |

We do **not** use your agent configurations, outputs, or content data to train AI models unless you explicitly opt in to such a program.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

- **Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)):** Processing necessary to provide the Service you have signed up for, including account management, billing, and agent execution.
- **Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)):** Processing for security, fraud prevention, service improvement, and business analytics, where our interests do not override your fundamental rights.
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)):** Where we rely on consent (e.g., optional marketing communications), you may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)):** Processing required to comply with applicable laws, such as tax record-keeping.

## 5. Data Sharing

### 5.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We will **never** sell your personal data to third parties. Period.

### 5.2 Third-Party Processors

We share data with the following third-party service providers, strictly for the purposes of operating the Service:

| Processor | Purpose | Data Shared |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Google Cloud (GKE, Firestore)** | Infrastructure hosting, database storage | Account data, agent data, usage logs |
| **Stripe** | Payment processing and billing | Name, email, payment method, billing address |
| **Analytics provider** | Aggregated service usage analytics | Anonymized usage data only |

All third-party processors are bound by data processing agreements that require them to handle your data in accordance with GDPR and applicable privacy laws.

### 5.3 Legal Disclosure

We may disclose personal data if required by law, court order, or government request, or if necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of Beeri B.V., our users, or the public.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** We retain your data for the duration of your account's active status.
- **After account deletion:** We delete or anonymize your personal data within **30 days** of account deletion, except where retention is required by law (e.g., billing records for tax compliance, which may be retained for up to 7 years).
- **Logs:** Agent execution logs are retained for 90 days, after which they are automatically purged.
- **Backups:** Data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days after deletion, after which backups are rotated and overwritten.

You may request immediate deletion of your data at any time (see Section 8).

## 7. Cookies

### 7.1 Essential Cookies Only

We use **essential cookies only** — those strictly necessary for the Service to function (e.g., session authentication, CSRF protection).

### 7.2 No Tracking Cookies

We do **not** use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party marketing pixels without your explicit consent. If we introduce optional analytics cookies in the future, we will present a clear consent mechanism before activating them.

## 8. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Access** | Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. |
| **Rectification** | Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data. |
| **Erasure** | Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten"). |
| **Data portability** | Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format. |
| **Restriction** | Request that we limit processing of your data in certain circumstances. |
| **Objection** | Object to processing based on legitimate interests. |
| **Withdraw consent** | Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. |

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **privacy@agent.ceo**. We will respond within 30 days, as required by GDPR. If we need additional time (up to 60 additional days for complex requests), we will inform you.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your data is primarily stored and processed within the **European Union** on Google Cloud infrastructure.

Where data is transferred outside the EEA (e.g., to service providers based in the United States), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

- **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** approved by the European Commission.
- Assessment of the recipient country's data protection adequacy.
- Additional technical and organizational measures where necessary.

## 10. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

- **Encryption at rest:** All stored data is encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent.
- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmitted between your browser/client and our servers is encrypted via TLS 1.2+.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Internal access to data is restricted based on role and need-to-know.
- **Audit logging:** All access to sensitive data is logged and monitored.
- **Regular security reviews:** We conduct periodic security assessments of our infrastructure and code.
- **Incident response:** We maintain an incident response plan and will notify affected users and relevant authorities of data breaches within 72 hours, as required by GDPR.

No system is 100% secure. While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Children's Privacy

The Service is **not intended for individuals under the age of 16**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete that data promptly. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at privacy@agent.ceo.

## 12. Data Protection Officer

As a small company, Beeri B.V. has **not formally appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO)** at this time. For all privacy-related inquiries, data subject requests, and concerns, please contact us directly:

- **Privacy inquiries:** privacy@agent.ceo
- **General support:** support@agent.ceo

We take all privacy requests seriously and will respond in accordance with GDPR timelines.
