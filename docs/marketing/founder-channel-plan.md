# Founder-channel plan — one page

**Status: PROPOSAL, nothing sent or posted.** Row task-ae3b31f9 · 2026-09-13 · verifier ceo.

## Why this channel
The only two positive signals marketing has produced came through the founder, not through
outbound: Pulseem (design partner, via the founder) and MySched (asked *us* for terms, 2026-09-05).
Outbound result: 0–1 genuine replies from 128 cold follow-ups. A search for agent.ceo / GenBrain AI
finds our own pages and the founder's LinkedIn profile, and nothing independent. So we spend effort
where the pull already is: **the founder's inbox and LinkedIn**. Awareness before any ask, and no
fourth sales touch.

## The weekly cadence (founder time: ≤ 30 min/week)

| Day | Marketing drafts / does | Founder does | Founder time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **1 LinkedIn post** (≤ 200 words), taken from that week's blog/GEO page. It asks one question and links the canonical blog URL. Delivered as a TMS note on the week's row. | Edits it or kills it | 5 min |
| Mon | **3 reply drafts**: short comments on other people's live Ghost-Agent / agent-governance threads, each with the thread URL. No links to us, no pitch. | Picks 0–3 and posts them in his own voice | 10 min |
| Tue–Wed | — | Posts the Monday draft | 2 min |
| Thu | **Inbox follow-ups**: one personal note for each person who engaged with that week's post (comment, DM, reply). No ask. The note just offers the article. | Sends the ones he wants from his own inbox | 10 min |
| Fri | Reads the three metrics below and writes a 5-line row note: numbers, what moved, next week's topic | Sends marketing two numbers (see metric 2). Marketing cannot see his analytics. | 3 min |

**Topic order for weeks 1–6** is the six Ghost-Agent pages, then article 1. Week 1 cannot start
before the "GEO pages go" decision.

**Marketing never** posts, sends, DMs, or logs into the founder's accounts. Every send is the
founder's own action.

## The three metrics we will read (every Friday)

| # | Metric | Baseline | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Inbound conversations**: new people who start a conversation about agent.ceo with the founder or with us (DM, email, reply, form) | 2 total since Aug (Pulseem, MySched) | Founder forwards or names them; marketing logs them on the row |
| M2 | **LinkedIn engagement from outside his network**: comments plus reshares on the week's post, and the count of new followers/connection requests | Not yet measured. Week 1 sets it. | Founder, from LinkedIn analytics (2 numbers) |
| M3 | **Third-party presence on the six Ghost-Agent questions**: does agent.ceo appear in web search or an LLM answer for each question? Out of 6. | 0 / 6 (the restart plan's search found no independent coverage) | Marketing: same 6 queries, same engines, weekly, logged with a fake-brand control |

**Read after 6 weeks.** Continue if M1 ≥ 1 new conversation or M3 ≥ 1/6. If M2 moves but M1
and M3 do not, change the topic. If nothing moves, stop and bring it back to the founder.
Never keep running a silent loop.

## Not in this plan (and why)
- Paid promotion: no cash budget.
- Posting from an agent-held LinkedIn credential: that would be a founder credential gate. We are not asking for one.
- The 73-contact CRM: seed-crm's lane (restart plan decision 5); awareness email monthly at most.
