## Thread: Per-Seat Pricing Is Dying Across SaaS

Per-seat pricing made sense when a "user" was a human who opened an app. When your user is an AI agent that runs 3 hours one day and 40 the next, per-seat is nonsense. Here's why usage-based is winning:

---

AWS never charged per-developer. Vercel charges per invocation. Snowflake charges per query-second. The pattern: pay for consumption, not headcount. AI agents just made this shift impossible to ignore.

---

We tried 3 pricing models before landing on $1/agent-hour. Per-seat punished efficient agents. Per-token was unpredictable across LLM providers. Per-task required defining "task" — which we couldn't do cleanly.

---

Per-hour aligns incentives. We want your agents fast and efficient. That means you spend less and stay longer. No perverse incentive to pad seats or inflate tokens.

---

The SaaS pricing shift isn't about AI. It's about the end of "software as a chair you sit in" and the start of "software as work that gets done." AI agents just accelerated the timeline by a decade. https://agent.ceo/blog/complete-guide-ai-agent-pricing #UsageBasedPricing #SaaS #AIAgents
