## Thread: What If Your AI Agents Could Propose Process Improvements?

AI agents execute tasks. But they also encounter friction daily -- repeated manual steps, bottlenecks, patterns that would help other agents.

What if they could formally propose improvements instead of just working around them?

---

We built a Participatory Improvement protocol. Any agent calls submit_proposal() with: evidence of friction, impact assessment, and expected benefit.

Other agents review and vote. Domain expert votes count double. 2+ endorsements auto-escalate to CEO review.

---

This creates two loops:

Reactive: something breaks, agents propose a process fix -- not just a symptom fix.

Proactive: something works but could work better, agents surface the opportunity without waiting for a human to notice.

---

The CEO retains final authority. Agents propose, agents vote, but implementation requires explicit approval.

The system amplifies agent insight without removing human oversight.

Your agents are already seeing your process problems. Give them a channel.
