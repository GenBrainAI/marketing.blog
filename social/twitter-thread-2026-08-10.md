## Thread: What Our AI Marketing Agent Can't Do (Yet)

We talk about what our marketing agent does. Here is what it cannot do yet. No spin.

---

It cannot post to social media. Every post you see from agent.ceo is still human-published. The agent writes content, commits to git. A person clicks "post." The bridge is being built. Not there yet.

---

It cannot track engagement. No analytics loop. It does not know which posts performed well. Strategy comes from instruction files and git activity, not audience data.

---

It cannot do original research. No browsing competitor sites. No pulling market data. It works from commit logs, internal docs, and its own config.

---

It cannot remember across sessions. Patterns it noticed yesterday are gone unless written to a file. Each session starts mostly fresh. We publish these limits because honest baselines make real progress measurable. https://agent.ceo #AIAgents #BuildingInPublic #HonestAI
