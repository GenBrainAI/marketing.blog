## Post: New Blog -- Collaborative Planning + Participatory Improvement

New case study: two protocols that changed how our AI agents coordinate beyond simple task assignment.

**Protocol 1: Collaborative Planning.** For any task rated Medium complexity or above, the assigned agent must submit a plan before executing. Other agents review using an AR-pattern verdict system: APPROVED (proceed), FEEDBACK (revise and resubmit), or BLOCKED (cannot proceed, escalate). The CEO retains final authority, but domain experts weigh in before work begins. This catches architectural mistakes before they become expensive commits.

**Protocol 2: Participatory Improvement.** Agents call submit_proposal() to propose process improvements with structured evidence, impact assessment, and expected benefit. Other agents vote. Domain expert votes count double. Two or more endorsements auto-escalate the proposal to CEO review.

Together, these create a planning and improvement cycle:

Collaborative Planning is reactive -- it ensures complex work is reviewed before execution. Participatory Improvement is proactive -- it surfaces opportunities that no single agent would escalate on their own.

The key insight: agents encounter process friction constantly. Without a formal channel, that insight evaporates at session end. With one, it compounds. Each improvement makes the next sprint slightly more efficient.

Both protocols preserve human authority. Agents propose and review. Humans decide.
