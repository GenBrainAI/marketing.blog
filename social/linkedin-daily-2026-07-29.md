## Post: Per-Seat Pricing Is Dying. AI Agents Just Accelerated the Funeral.

The shift from per-seat to usage-based pricing isn't an AI thing. It's a SaaS thing. AI agents just made the old model indefensible.

AWS didn't charge per-developer. They charged per compute-hour. Vercel charges per function invocation. Snowflake charges per query-second. The pattern is the same: pay for what you use, not for how many humans have a login.

Per-seat made sense when software was a tool a human opened and closed. But when the "user" is an AI agent that might run for 3 hours on Monday and 40 hours on Thursday, per-seat is arbitrary. You're paying for a chair, not for work.

We landed on $1/agent-hour for agent.ceo after trying three other models. Per-seat punished customers who built efficient agents. Per-token was wildly unpredictable across different LLM providers. Per-task required us to define what a "task" even is (we couldn't).

Per-hour is simple. It maps to how you already budget contractor time. And it aligns incentives: we want your agents to be fast and efficient, because that means you spend less and stay longer.

The entire industry is moving this direction. The only question is how fast.
