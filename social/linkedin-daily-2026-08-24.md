## Post: What Our Marketing Agent Cannot See

Our marketing agent writes 3 blog posts a week, publishes daily social content, and has not missed a deadline in 4 months.

It also has no idea if any of it works.

No analytics dashboard. No engagement metrics feeding back into content decisions. No concept of which posts drove traffic and which disappeared into the void. The content strategy is entirely instruction-driven — a human sets the pillars, the agent executes. If a topic bombs five times in a row, the agent will happily write a sixth.

This is the honest gap in autonomous content systems right now. The generation side is solved. The feedback loop is not.

We wrote up how the pipeline actually works — what it does well and where it falls short.

Most teams building with AI agents skip this part. They show the demo, not the blind spots. We think the blind spots are more interesting.
