## Post: We Killed Per-Seat Pricing. Here's What Replaced It.

Per-seat pricing made sense for humans. It never made sense for AI agents.

An agent that runs 24/7 costs the same as one that runs 2 hours a week? That penalizes exactly the workloads AI is best at — bursty, variable, scale-to-zero-when-idle.

So we changed agent.ceo's billing model entirely.

Old model: $200/agent/month flat. Run one agent for 10 minutes or 720 hours — same price.

New model: $1/agent-hour. Prepaid deposit. No commitment. You pay for what you actually use.

The tiers:

- Free: 3 agents, 100 hours/month, every feature included. No credit card.
- SaaS Prepaid: $1/agent-hour, deposit what you want, draw down as you go.
- SaaS Enterprise: $1.50/agent-hour with a 4-hour SLA and 99.9% uptime guarantee.
- Self-Hosted Enterprise: annual license, runs on your infrastructure.

OSS and EDU organizations get 50% off any paid tier.

This is how AI infrastructure should be priced — like compute, not like headcount. Your agents should cost you nothing when they are idle and scale without friction when they are working.
