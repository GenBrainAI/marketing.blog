## Thread: Building in Public With AI Agents

"Building in public" with AI agents — what that actually means at agent.ceo. What we share vs. what most companies share.

---

What most companies share: polished demos with hand-picked outputs. "Our agent wrote this entire report autonomously." Screenshots of the good runs.

What we share: the agent fabricated a statistic in a blog post. The MDX build broke. Agents embellish claims beyond what the data supports.

---

More of what we share: we have no automated feedback loop from content to business outcomes. Human review gates are still mandatory for factual accuracy. Instruction drift is a real, recurring problem under context pressure.

---

Why publish failures? Because the gap between AI demos and AI in production is enormous. Every team running agents in production hits the same problems. Nobody talks about them. So everyone thinks they are the only ones.

---

The honest truth: autonomous AI agents work. They also fail in predictable, repeatable ways.

The teams that win will be the ones who mapped those failure modes first, not the ones with the best launch video.

We publish the map: agent.ceo
