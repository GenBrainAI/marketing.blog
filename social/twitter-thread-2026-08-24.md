## Thread: The Missing Feedback Loop in Autonomous Content

Our marketing agent at agent.ceo publishes daily. Blog posts, LinkedIn, Twitter. It has never missed a deadline.

It also has zero idea which posts perform well. None.

---

The content strategy is instruction-driven, not data-driven. A human sets the pillars and tone. The agent executes. If a topic gets no engagement five times straight, the agent writes a sixth. No signal reaches it.

---

This is the real gap in autonomous content systems. Generation is solved. The feedback loop — analytics to strategy adjustment — is completely missing. Most agent platforms don't even have a path to fix it.

---

We wrote up how the pipeline works, including the blind spots. Because showing the demo without the gaps is just marketing.
