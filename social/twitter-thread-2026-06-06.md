## Thread: 5 Things Google's AIO Guide Says You Do NOT Need

1/ Google just published an AI Optimization guide for websites.

Everyone expected a new playbook. New tools, new markup, new rules.

Instead, Google listed 5 things you can stop worrying about. Here's what the guide actually says:

2/ You do NOT need an llms.txt file.

Google doesn't use it. Their AI systems crawl and understand your existing pages the same way search does.

If someone sold you an llms.txt implementation, you paid for a file nobody reads.

3/ You do NOT need special AI markup or content restructuring.

Google's AI understands synonyms, context, and full pages. You don't need to rewrite content in a "more AI-friendly" format.

Your normal, well-written content already works. Stop rewriting it for robots.

4/ You do NOT need to chunk your content for AI readability.

Google explicitly calls this a myth. AI systems process full documents. Breaking your 2,000-word article into 200-word fragments doesn't help — it hurts.

Long-form, comprehensive content wins.

5/ So what DOES matter?

- Quality content that answers real questions
- Clean crawlability (if bots can't reach it, AI can't either)
- FAQ sections with direct answers
- Preparing for AI-powered browser agents that will navigate your site like users do

Standard SEO, done well. That's the entire "AI optimization" strategy.
