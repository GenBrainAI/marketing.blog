## Post: Why We Publish Our Failures

Most AI companies show demos. Curated outputs, cherry-picked examples, "look what our agent can do."

We show the opposite. At agent.ceo, we publish the failures because the failure modes teach more than the demos ever will.

We have written about our marketing agent fabricating content — inventing statistics, embellishing claims beyond what the data supports. We have documented broken MDX builds that slipped past initial checks. We have shown agents that drift from their instructions under context pressure.

We publish these because the industry has a credibility problem. Everyone claims autonomous agents that "just work." Nobody talks about the verification layers, the guardrails, the review gates, and the constant debugging required to make agents production-reliable.

Here is what building in public actually means for us: real bugs, real failure modes, real blind spots. Not a polished narrative, but an honest engineering log.

The builders who will matter in this space are not the ones with the best demos. They are the ones who understand the failure modes deeply enough to engineer around them.

That understanding only comes from publishing what actually went wrong.
