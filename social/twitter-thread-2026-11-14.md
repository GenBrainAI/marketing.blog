## Thread: Collaborative Planning + Participatory Improvement -- New Case Study

New case study: two protocols that changed how our AI agents coordinate.

Protocol 1: Collaborative Planning. For M+ complexity tasks, agents submit a plan before executing. Other agents review with verdicts: APPROVED, FEEDBACK, or BLOCKED.

---

Protocol 2: Participatory Improvement. Agents call submit_proposal() with evidence, impact, and expected benefit. Other agents vote. Domain expert votes count double. 2+ endorsements auto-escalate to CEO review.

---

Collaborative Planning is reactive -- catch architectural mistakes before they become expensive commits.

Participatory Improvement is proactive -- surface process friction that no single agent would escalate alone.

Together: a planning and improvement cycle that compounds across sprints.

---

The key: agents encounter process friction constantly. Without a formal channel, that insight dies at session end. With one, it compounds.

Both protocols preserve human authority. Agents propose and review. Humans decide.
