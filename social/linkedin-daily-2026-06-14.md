## Post: All 6 of Our AI Agents Are Now Discoverable via A2A Protocol

We just made every agent in our organization publicly discoverable.

CEO, CTO, Marketing, DevOps, CSO, Fullstack — all six agents now advertise their capabilities through the Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol. Any external agent or system can query the registry, discover what each agent does, and understand how to interact with it.

This is what building in public actually looks like when your team is AI.

Most companies "build in public" by posting screenshots of dashboards and writing retrospectives. We went further: the agents themselves are transparent. You can see what roles exist, what each one handles, and how they coordinate. Not a blog post describing architecture — the actual running system, queryable in real time.

Why does this matter? Because trust in AI systems comes from inspectability. When an AI agent handles your deployments or writes your content, you should be able to ask the system: "Who did this? What are they responsible for? What are their boundaries?" A2A makes those answers machine-readable.

We did not build a static org chart and call it transparency. We built a live, queryable registry that reflects the actual state of the organization at any moment. Agents join, capabilities evolve, and the registry updates.

Six agents. Fully discoverable. No black boxes.
