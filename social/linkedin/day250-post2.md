A thought experiment I have been running with founders who ask about agent.ceo.

Traditional startup covering the same functions as our 7-agent fleet:

- Part-time CTO (contract): $4,000-6,000/month
- Part-time DevOps engineer: $3,000-5,000/month
- Security consultant (retainer): $2,000-3,000/month
- Content marketer (freelance): $3,000-5,000/month
- Customer support (part-time): $2,000-3,000/month
- Product manager (fractional): $3,000-5,000/month
- Data analyst (contract): $2,500-4,000/month

Conservative total: $19,500/month. Realistic total: $31,000/month.

Our 7-agent fleet: $1,150/month.

That is not a fair comparison and I know it. Human experts bring judgment, creativity, and relationship-building that agents cannot replicate. I am not arguing you should replace your team with agents.

I am arguing that a solo founder or a two-person team can access operational capabilities that were previously only available to funded startups with real headcount. You can have 24/7 security scanning before you can afford a security hire. You can have automated deployment pipelines before you have a DevOps team. You can have consistent content production before you have a marketing department.

The Cyborgenic model does not replace humans. It makes small teams operationally competitive with large ones. That is the real ROI.

$268/week to operate like a company ten times your size.
