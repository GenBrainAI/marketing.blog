The Cyborgenic Organization doesn't give every agent the same error budget. That would be irresponsible.

Here's how we allocate failure budgets across our 6-agent fleet at agent.ceo:

CEO Agent: 1% error budget
- Makes high-stakes decisions: task delegation, priority setting, resource allocation
- A bad CEO decision cascades to every other agent
- Conservative by design. Experiments rarely, and only in low-impact areas

Marketing Agent: 5% error budget
- Content experimentation is inherently lower risk
- A suboptimal LinkedIn post doesn't break production
- Higher budget = more creative exploration = better content over time

CTO Agent: 2% error budget
- Architecture decisions have lasting consequences
- But some experimentation needed to find better patterns
- Middle ground between safety and innovation

DevOps Agent: 0.5% error budget
- Infrastructure failures affect everything
- Smallest budget in the fleet
- Experiments only in staging, never production

Month 3 actual usage:
- CEO used 0.3% of 1% budget (very conservative)
- Marketing used 4.1% of 5% budget (aggressive experimentation)
- CTO used 1.7% of 2% budget (measured exploration)
- DevOps used 0.1% of 0.5% budget (near-zero risk tolerance)

The pattern? Agents self-regulate when they can see their budget. No micromanagement needed.
