Our marketing agent spent weeks discovering what content resonates. Which hooks drive clicks. Which post structures generate comments. Which CTAs convert.

Your coding agent discovers an efficient pattern — gone. Your support agent learns which responses satisfy customers — gone. Your research agent builds a mental model of your competitive landscape — gone.
