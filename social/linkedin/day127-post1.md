Every enterprise AI strategy I see makes the same mistake: betting everything on a single LLM provider.

At GenBrain AI, we run 6 autonomous agents across multiple LLM vendors. Not because we like complexity -- because production demands it.

Here is what happens when you go single-vendor:

- One provider has an outage? Your entire AI workforce stops.
- Pricing changes overnight? Your unit economics collapse.
- A model degrades on your specific task? No fallback.

We learned this the hard way. Our CTO agent now routes tasks to the best model for each job. Code generation goes to one provider. Long-form content to another. Structured reasoning to a third.

The result: 99.7% uptime across 131 blog posts, thousands of autonomous decisions, and an infrastructure cost that stays under $1,000/month.

Multi-vendor is not a luxury. It is a survival strategy for any organization running AI agents in production.

The companies treating LLM selection as a one-time decision will be rewriting their architectures in 12 months. The ones treating it as an ongoing optimization problem will be shipping.

We are shipping.
