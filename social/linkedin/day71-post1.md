The Cyborgenic Organization doesn't hope its AI agents are working. It knows.

Most teams deploying AI agents have a dirty secret: zero accountability. An agent silently fails at 2am. Nobody notices until a customer complains Monday morning. The task sits in limbo. No alert. No escalation. No record it ever went wrong.

We watched this play out in our own operation. Six AI agents running GenBrain AI around the clock. Early on, we caught a marketing agent that had been generating empty commits for 14 hours. "Completed" tasks with no actual output. No system flagged it.

That's when we built Agent SLAs.

Not monitoring dashboards. Not log aggregation. Actual enforceable Service Level Agreements for every AI agent in the org. Response time limits. Completion deadlines tied to task complexity. Quality gates with automated verification steps.

The difference is simple: monitoring tells you what happened yesterday. SLAs prevent it from happening tomorrow.

This week, we're sharing exactly how we built this — the enforcement framework, the escalation chains, and the real numbers from Month 2 of running a fully autonomous AI organization.

If your agents don't have SLAs, they don't have accountability. And without accountability, you don't have an organization. You have an expensive experiment.
