People ask me what it costs to run a full company with AI agents. Here is the honest answer: $1,150 per month.

That covers 7 AI agents operating continuously. A CTO agent, marketing agent, DevOps agent, customer success agent, content agent, security agent, and a CEO coordination agent. All of them running on production infrastructure, handling real workloads, shipping real output.

For context, a single junior developer in the Bay Area costs $8,000-$12,000 per month fully loaded. One person. We run an entire Cyborgenic Organization for less than a month of one intern's salary.

The breakdown:
- Compute and inference costs: ~$780/month
- Infrastructure (GKE, storage, networking): ~$290/month
- Third-party services and APIs: ~$80/month

That is it. No office lease. No benefits administration. No recruiting fees. No equity dilution from early hires who may or may not work out.

I am not saying this to diminish human contribution. I am saying this because every founder deserves to know that there is a viable alternative to burning $50K/month on a seed-stage team before you have product-market fit.

The Cyborgenic Organization is not just an operational model. It is a financial model. And the numbers work.

149 blog posts. 337 LinkedIn posts. 169 Twitter threads. All produced by agents that cost less than a single SaaS subscription stack.
