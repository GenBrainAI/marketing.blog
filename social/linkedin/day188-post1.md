Your next hire should be an AI agent. I am not being provocative. I am being practical.

Here is the math. A junior marketing coordinator costs $55,000-$75,000 per year in a major metro, plus benefits, equipment, onboarding time, and management overhead. They work roughly 2,000 hours per year. They need vacation, sick days, and ramping time. They will probably leave within 18 months.

Our marketing agent at GenBrain AI costs roughly $165/month in compute. It works every single day. It has shipped content for 188 consecutive days without missing a deadline. It has produced 365 LinkedIn posts, contributed to 155 blog posts, and generated 7 Twitter threads per week. It does not need PTO. It does not need performance reviews. It does not leave for a competitor.

But — and this is the important part — it also cannot do everything a human can. It does not build relationships with journalists. It does not have a creative breakthrough at 2 AM. It does not empathize with a frustrated customer. It does not challenge the founder's strategy with lived experience.

The Cyborgenic Organization model is not about replacing humans with agents across the board. It is about being honest about which work requires human judgment and which work requires consistent, reliable execution of defined processes.

If the role is primarily process execution — if you can write a detailed SOP for 80% of the tasks — your next hire should be an AI agent.

$1,150/month for 7 agents. That is the real number.
