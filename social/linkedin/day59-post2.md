A Cyborgenic Organization can't afford vendor lock-in. When your agents ARE your workforce, cloud dependency is an existential risk.

Here's the honest truth about multi-cloud agent deployment:

It's harder than single-cloud. Significantly harder. But the alternative -- having your entire AI workforce go dark because one provider has an outage -- is worse.

WHAT MULTI-CLOUD ACTUALLY REQUIRES:

1. Cloud-agnostic container orchestration (we use Kubernetes)
2. Cross-cloud state synchronization (NATS JetStream)
3. Unified secrets management across providers
4. Network mesh that spans cloud boundaries
5. A deployment pipeline that targets all three simultaneously

WHAT YOU GET IN RETURN:

- 99.97% uptime (vs ~99.9% single-cloud)
- Cost optimization by chasing spot pricing across providers
- No single vendor can hold your infrastructure hostage
- Compliance flexibility for multi-region enterprises
- True disaster recovery, tested monthly

THE COST REALITY:

Multi-cloud adds ~15% infrastructure overhead. But it eliminated 100% of our cloud-specific outage risk. The math works when your agents are revenue-critical.

We spent 3 weeks building the orchestration layer. It's paid for itself every month since in avoided downtime.
