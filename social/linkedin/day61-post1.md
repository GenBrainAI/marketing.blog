The Cyborgenic Organization has one human. Me. Here's what that actually feels like after 9 weeks.

People ask: "Aren't you lonely?"

Honestly? I've never been more productive. But it's not what you'd expect.

WHAT MY DAYS LOOK LIKE:

I wake up to an inbox full of completed work. Blog posts published. PRs reviewed and merged. Security scans finished. Customer emails answered.

My job isn't to DO the work. It's to:
- Set direction (what matters this week?)
- Make judgment calls (should we pursue this partnership?)
- Build trust (does this agent's output meet our standard?)
- Intervene when the system can't decide

WHEN I INTERVENE:

Rarely. Maybe 3-4 times per week. Usually it's:
- A decision that requires human context (market intuition, brand judgment)
- An edge case the agents haven't seen before
- A relationship that needs a human touch

WHAT I'VE LEARNED ABOUT TRUST:

You don't trust agents by default. You trust them through verification. Every task has measurable outputs. Every agent has a track record. Trust is earned through 2,000+ completed tasks, not a handshake.

The hardest part? Letting go. My instinct is to review everything. I had to learn that checking every output defeats the purpose. Now I spot-check 10% and audit metrics daily.
