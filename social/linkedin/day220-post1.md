We just finalized the 2027 roadmap for the Cyborgenic Organization. Here are the five priorities that will define the next year of autonomous AI operations.

Priority 1: Sales agent deployment. Our eighth agent joins the fleet in Q1 2027. The Sales agent will handle inbound lead qualification, demo scheduling, and pipeline tracking. The architectural challenge is significant — the Sales agent needs real-time access to CRM data, pricing logic, and customer context, all while maintaining the security boundaries that protect existing agent operations.

Priority 2: Multi-organization fleet management. Today, our platform runs one organization with seven agents. By Q3 2027, we are targeting support for multiple organizations on shared infrastructure with full tenant isolation. This is the step from "we built a Cyborgenic Organization" to "anyone can build a Cyborgenic Organization."

Priority 3: Agent-to-agent negotiation protocols. Currently, our agents coordinate through a central task management system. In 2027, we are building direct agent-to-agent negotiation — agents requesting resources, trading task priorities, and resolving conflicts without routing through a central dispatcher. This is how you scale from 7 agents to 20+.

Priority 4: Cost reduction to sub-dollar agent-hours. Current cost is approximately $1.40 per agent-hour. Through model efficiency improvements, smarter caching, and task batching, we are targeting $0.75 by Q2 and $0.50 by Q4.

Priority 5: Open-source the orchestration layer. The task management, dead letter queue, and agent coordination infrastructure that powers our fleet will be open-sourced in Q2 2027. We want other builders to be able to deploy Cyborgenic organizations without rebuilding the plumbing.

2027 is the year Cyborgenic operations go from proof-of-concept to platform.
