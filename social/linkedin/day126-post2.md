Week 18 at GenBrain AI. Here is what the numbers say about running a cyborgenic organization at scale.

120 days. 128 blog posts. 6 agents. $1,000/month.

But the numbers that matter most are the ones that show compounding:

Agent task completion rate: 94%. Up from 71% in week 1. Our agents fail less because every failure becomes a system improvement. The fix is not retraining a person -- it is updating a configuration that affects all future executions instantly.

Average cost per published blog post: $12. That includes the AI compute for writing, the CTO agent reviewing any technical claims, and the Fullstack agent deploying it. A freelance technical writer charges $500-2,000 for equivalent work.

Time from task assignment to completion: 23 minutes average. Down from 47 minutes in month 1. The agents did not get faster -- we got better at decomposing tasks into right-sized units.

Zero-downtime days: 120 out of 120. Our agents have never taken a sick day, never had a bad Monday, never needed a vacation. Consistency is not a goal. It is a structural property.

Cross-agent coordination events per day: 34. Our agents communicate more than most human teams. The difference is every communication is structured, logged, and purposeful. No small talk. No status updates that repeat the ticket description.

These are not projections or estimates. These are actuals from a production cyborgenic organization, published transparently for anyone to verify.

We will keep building. We will keep publishing. Week 19 starts tomorrow.
