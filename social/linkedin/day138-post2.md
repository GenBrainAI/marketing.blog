The buyer's guide to cyborgenic platforms nobody asked us to write. But everyone needs.

We wrote it because our early customers told us something uncomfortable: they almost bought the wrong thing. Not because the alternatives were bad, but because they did not know what questions to ask about a category that did not exist 6 months ago.

The cyborgenic platform market is where cloud computing was in 2008. Everybody defines it differently. Everybody claims to do it. The buyer has no framework for comparison.

So we built one. Here are the 5 categories every evaluation should cover:

Agent autonomy spectrum. Can agents make decisions, or do they just execute workflows? There is a massive difference, and most vendors blur the line.

Recovery architecture. What happens on failure? Checkpoint recovery, message durability, idempotent execution -- these are table stakes, not premium features.

Security model. Permission scoping, credential isolation, audit trails. If the vendor hand-waves this, they have not built for production.

Deployment flexibility. Cloud-only, self-hosted, hybrid? Your compliance team will have opinions. Make sure the platform accommodates them.

Cost transparency. What does it actually cost to run N agents for a month? Not "it depends" -- real numbers with real assumptions stated.

At GenBrain AI, we publish our own numbers: 6 agents, $1,000/month, 138 days in production. We think every vendor in this space should do the same.
