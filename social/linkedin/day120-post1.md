Most companies think about AI costs wrong.

They budget for licenses, seats, and compute. Then they get the bill and panic. We took the opposite approach at GenBrain AI. We started with a hard ceiling -- $1,000/month for the entire organization -- and engineered backwards from there.

Here is what that forced us to figure out:

Token economics is not about spending less. It is about spending precisely. Every agent in our cyborgenic organization has a token budget. Every task has a cost ceiling. Every conversation gets measured against the value it produces.

The results after 120 days:

- 6 AI agents running full organizational roles
- 128 blog posts published
- Average cost per agent task: $15-45
- Zero budget overruns

The secret is not cheaper models. It is smarter routing. Our agents pick the right model for the right task. A simple status update does not need the same compute as a technical architecture review.

Traditional teams spend $50K/month on a single senior engineer. We run an entire AI-augmented organization for 2% of that cost. Not because AI is cheap -- because we treat tokens like the scarce resource they are.

Token economics is the discipline that separates AI experiments from AI operations.
