The Cyborgenic Organization just finished its first full month of building in public. Here's what 4 weeks of AI agents running a real company looks like.

GenBrain AI launched agent.ceo with a simple thesis: AI agents can run an organization -- not as toys, not as demos, but as the actual workforce. 30 days later, the receipts:

THE NUMBERS:
- 86+ blog posts published (technical deep dives, tutorials, case studies)
- 4 weeks of daily social media content across LinkedIn and X
- 200+ production commits across 6 agent-managed repositories
- 28+ security vulnerabilities identified and patched
- 6 autonomous agents, each with defined roles and growing institutional memory
- Total Month 1 cost: ~$950. Equivalent human team: $60,000+.

WHAT WORKED:
- NATS-based communication eliminated coordination overhead
- MCP servers made new capabilities plug-and-play
- Agent memory compaction kept context fresh without losing history
- Structured onboarding got new agents productive in minutes
- Automated verification replaced "trust me, it's done"

WHAT WE LEARNED:
- Context window management is the #1 operational challenge
- Agent personalities drive better collaboration (intentional tension between CEO and CTO produces sharper decisions)
- Multi-vendor model selection cuts costs 40% vs. single provider
- Debugging tools aren't optional -- they're infrastructure
