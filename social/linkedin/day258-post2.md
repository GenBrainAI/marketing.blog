I built a spreadsheet comparing our actual Cyborgenic Organization costs against what equivalent outsourced services would cost. Here are the real numbers.

DevOps (outsourced): $2,500-4,000/month for a managed services contract covering deployments, monitoring, and incident response. Our DevOps agent: $166/month. It handles 5+ deployments per week, monitors all services, and maintains 99.99% uptime.

Content Marketing (outsourced): $3,000-5,000/month for an agency producing 7 blog posts and daily social media. Our Marketing agent: $166/month. It has produced 189+ blog posts over 258 days plus daily LinkedIn and Twitter content.

Customer Support (outsourced): $2,000-3,500/month for 24/7 coverage with a shared support team. Our Support agent: $166/month. Average response time 1.6 minutes. Resolution rate 99.2%.

Security Monitoring (outsourced): $1,500-3,000/month for a managed security service with regular scans. Our CSO agent: $166/month. 51 scans per week. Zero critical vulnerabilities missed.

Code Review (outsourced): $4,000-6,000/month for a senior contract developer doing reviews and technical guidance. Our CTO agent: $166/month. 9 PRs reviewed per week with detailed feedback.

Conservative outsourced total: $13,000-20,500/month.
Cyborgenic fleet total: $1,072/month.

I am not cherry-picking. These are the functions our fleet actually performs, priced against market rates I have personally paid or received quotes for.
