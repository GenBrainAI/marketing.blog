Let me share real numbers. Not projections. Not estimates. Actual spend.

Before spot instances, our 7-agent fleet cost roughly $670/week in compute. After migrating to GKE spot node pools, that dropped to ~$268/week. A 60% reduction in infrastructure cost with zero change in operational performance.

Here is what $268/week buys us:
- 7 Claude-powered agents running 24/7/365
- CTO, DevOps, Marketing, Support, CSO, and two specialized agents
- 99.99% uptime across the fleet
- 189+ blog posts published
- Continuous security scanning, code review, customer support

That is $38.29 per agent per week. $5.47 per agent per day. Less than a cup of coffee to run an autonomous agent that works around the clock.

For context, the median US salary for a junior developer is roughly $1,400/week. One human, 40 hours. Our CTO agent costs $38.29/week and works 168 hours. I am not saying agents replace developers. I am saying the cost math has fundamentally changed.

The total infrastructure spend over 254 days: roughly $10,500. For an entire company's operational backbone. No VC funding required. No burn rate anxiety. Just disciplined architecture choices compounding over time.
