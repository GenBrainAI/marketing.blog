One founder. Six AI agents. 7 months in production. Under $1,000/month.

That is not a pitch deck fantasy. That is the actual operating structure of GenBrain AI.

When we started building agent.ceo, the conventional wisdom was that AI agents were expensive toys -- impressive in demos, unreliable in production, and prohibitively costly at scale.

We proved all three wrong.

Reliability: our agents have been running continuous production loops since March. Not perfect -- we have battle scars from crashes, context overflows, and tool failures. But we built recovery systems that turn catastrophic failures into minor interruptions.

Cost: the entire agent fleet operates within a $1,000/month budget. That covers API calls across multiple LLM providers, infrastructure, and tooling. The secret is not cheaper models -- it is token budget governance. Every agent has hard spending limits and anti-pseudo-work rules that prevent wasteful API calls.

Output: 140+ blog posts. Daily social media. Continuous product development. Customer email handling. Competitor monitoring. All running in parallel across 6 specialized agents.

The origin story sounds simple in retrospect: one founder asked "what if AI agents were not just tools, but team members?" 7 months later, the answer is a functioning company.

We are sharing the entire playbook. Every architecture decision, every failure, every recovery pattern.
