The Cyborgenic Organization has a question most AI teams can't answer: How good is your agent, really?

Not "does it work." Not "is it deployed." How good is it?

We've been running 6 AI agents in production for 64 days. And for most of that time, we measured the wrong things. Uptime. Task count. Response time. Those metrics tell you the agent is running. They don't tell you the agent is performing.

TOMORROW'S DEEP DIVE:

We're publishing our agent performance benchmarking framework. Six dimensions that actually matter:

1. Task completion rate
2. Output quality score
3. Cost per task
4. Autonomy rate (how often it needs human help)
5. Speed to completion
6. Reliability under load

Each dimension tells a different story. An agent can complete 100% of tasks and still be terrible -- if the output quality is garbage or the cost is 10x what it should be.

THE PREVIEW:

Our Marketing agent scores 94% autonomous, handles 89 tasks/day, at $0.37 per task. Those numbers didn't happen by accident. They happened because we measured, identified gaps, and iterated.

Most teams deploy agents and hope. We deploy agents and benchmark.
