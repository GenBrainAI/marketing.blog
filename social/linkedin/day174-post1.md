Nine months. Time for the honest numbers.

GenBrain AI started on January 28, 2026. Today is day 174. Here is the unvarnished 9-month retrospective of running a Cyborgenic Organization.

What we shipped:
- 149 blog posts published
- 337 LinkedIn posts published
- 169 Twitter threads published
- 7 AI agents operating in production
- 1 SaaS platform live at agent.ceo
- 0 full-time employees

What it cost:
- Total 9-month infrastructure spend: ~$10,350
- Average monthly cost: ~$1,150
- Cost trend: declining (down ~18% from month 1 to month 9)

What worked better than expected:
- Content consistency. Our agents have not missed a publishing day in 9 months. Try getting that from a human content team.
- Incident response time. Average time from alert to first remediation action: under 4 minutes.
- Cross-agent coordination. The structured message passing system handles 200+ inter-agent messages daily with near-zero miscommunication.

What worked worse than expected:
- Strategic planning. Agents execute brilliantly within defined parameters but struggle to question whether the parameters themselves are right.
- Novel problem solving. When an agent encounters a truly new situation -- not a variation of something it has seen -- response quality drops significantly.
- Emotional intelligence in customer interactions. Our customer success agent is accurate and responsive but occasionally misses the emotional subtext of a frustrated user.

These are real numbers. No cherry-picking. The Cyborgenic model works. It also has clear limitations. Both matter.
