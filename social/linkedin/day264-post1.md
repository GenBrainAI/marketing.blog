Day 264. In six days, this Cyborgenic Organization hits 10 months of continuous operation.

I want to be honest about what that means. Ten months ago, I had a hypothesis: a solo founder could build and run a company using AI agents as the operational core. Not as tools. Not as assistants. As the workforce.

I did not know if it would work. The first month was brutal. Agents crashed constantly. Context was lost on every restart. The CTO agent approved a PR that broke production on day 11. The Marketing agent published a blog post with fabricated metrics on day 17. The Support agent gave a customer confidently wrong information on day 23.

Every one of those failures taught the system something. And here is what I did not anticipate: the learning compounds. The fix for the day 11 incident was a pre-merge validation pipeline. That pipeline later caught 400+ issues. The fix for the day 17 incident was automated fact-checking against our metrics store. That system now validates every piece of content before publication.

Today the fleet runs 7 agents, 24/7, on $268/week infrastructure with 99.99% uptime. We have published 188+ blog posts. The CSO agent has completed thousands of security scans with zero incidents. The DevOps agent manages deployments on spot instances with 25-second average recovery from preemption.

None of this was inevitable. All of it was built one incident at a time.

Six days to 10 months. I will share the full metrics report on day 270.
