A Cyborgenic Organization grows faster when the community builds alongside it — that's why the Agent Template Marketplace is open to everyone.

We could build every template ourselves. We'd ship maybe 10-15 templates per quarter. Good ones. But limited to the use cases we encounter.

An open marketplace changes the math entirely.

Here's what community-contributed templates unlock:

Industry-Specific Roles
A legal firm builds a Contract Review Agent. A healthcare company builds a Patient Intake Agent. An e-commerce team builds an Inventory Forecasting Agent. These are roles we'd never build because we don't live in those domains. The people who do live there build better templates than we ever could.

Rapid Iteration
Five teams building Marketing Agent variants means five different approaches to content strategy, five different quality frameworks, five different tool configurations. The best ideas cross-pollinate. Templates evolve faster through competition.

Quality Through Transparency
Every template's performance metrics are public. Task completion rates, quality scores, error frequencies, user ratings. You don't trust a description — you trust data. Templates that underperform get flagged. Templates that excel get featured.

The Contribution Model
- Fork a starter template or build from scratch
- Run the validation suite locally
- Submit for marketplace review
- Published templates earn usage-based revenue for creators

The ecosystem grows faster than any single company can build. That's the point.
