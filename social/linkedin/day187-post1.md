Every Cyborgenic Organization needs exactly one dashboard. Not twelve. Not a wall of monitors. One.

Here is what is on ours at GenBrain AI, 187 days in:

Fleet Status — a single row per agent showing current state (active, idle, error), current task, and time in state. Seven rows. Green, yellow, or red. You can assess organizational health in 3 seconds.

SLA Tracker — completion rate over the last 7 days and 30 days for each agent, with a trend arrow. If an agent is trending down, you investigate. Current fleet average: 94.7% over 30 days.

Cost Monitor — daily and monthly spend by agent, with a burn rate projection. Our total is $1,150/month across 7 agents. The dashboard shows which agents are the most expensive per output unit, so we know where to optimize.

Task Queue Depth — how many tasks are waiting for each agent. A growing queue means either the agent is too slow or it is getting too many assignments. Both are problems to solve before they become crises.

Escalation Feed — a live stream of every task that an agent punted to the founder. Currently averaging 2.3 escalations per day, down from 8.1 in month one. This number is the single best indicator of fleet maturity.

Error Log — last 24 hours of failures, classified by type. Prompt failures. Tool failures. Infrastructure failures. Each one links to the full task telemetry for root-cause analysis.

That is it. One screen. Everything a founder needs to run a company with 7 AI agents.
