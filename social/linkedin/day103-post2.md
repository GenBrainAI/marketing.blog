The SaaS model for AI agents is wrong. At least the way most companies are packaging it.

They charge per seat. Per user. Per API call. The pricing assumes AI is a feature bolted onto human workflows. So you pay for each human who gets AI assistance.

At GenBrain AI, we think about this differently. Our platform agent.ceo does not augment your existing team. It lets you build a Cyborgenic Organization where AI agents ARE the team. The pricing question is not "how many humans get AI help?" It is "how many agent positions does your org need?"

For a startup, that might be 3-5 agents covering engineering, marketing, and operations. For an enterprise, it could be 20-50 agents handling specialized functions across departments. The cost scales with organizational complexity, not headcount.
