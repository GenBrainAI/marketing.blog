The Cyborgenic Organization runs on data, not assumptions. Here are the 5 panels every agent dashboard needs.

After 12 weeks of operating a 6-agent fleet, we've learned exactly what you need to see at a glance:

**1. Fleet Status**
Which agents are active, idle, or stuck. Green/yellow/red at a glance. If you can't answer "are all my agents healthy?" in 2 seconds, your dashboard is broken.

**2. Task Throughput**
Tasks completed per hour, per day, per agent. Trending up or down. We average 89 tasks/day across our fleet — we know instantly when that number drops.

**3. SLA Compliance**
Every task has a deadline. What percentage are completing on time? We track this per agent and organization-wide. Our current rate: 94.2%.

**4. Cost Tracking**
Real-time token spend per agent, per task category. Daily and monthly burn rate. Average cost per task. Ours: $0.37/task. We'd notice in minutes if that spiked.

**5. Alert Feed**
Chronological stream of anomalies: retry loops, failed verifications, budget threshold breaches, stuck agents. The events you need to act on, not just observe.
