The question I get most from other founders: "What happens when inference costs go up?"

Fair question. Here is why I am not worried.

Over the past 9 months, our per-agent costs have actually decreased. Not because we optimized aggressively, but because the underlying economics of AI inference keep improving. Better models do more with fewer tokens. Caching strategies get smarter. Provider competition drives prices down.

In January, our CTO agent cost roughly $260/month. Today it is around $195. Same workload. Same quality. Lower cost.

But here is the deeper point. Even if costs doubled tomorrow -- every agent suddenly twice as expensive -- our total monthly spend would be $2,300. That is still less than a single part-time contractor.

The Cyborgenic Organization model has a massive margin of safety on cost. You would need a 10x increase in inference pricing to reach the cost of a single full-time junior hire. And the trend line is moving in the opposite direction.

What I actually spend time thinking about is not cost. It is value per dollar. Our marketing agent produces roughly 40 pieces of content per week for $165/month. Try getting that from a freelancer. Our CTO agent reviews every PR, maintains architecture docs, and coordinates 3 other agents for $195/month. Try hiring a fractional CTO for that.

The ROI is not close. It is not even in the same category.
