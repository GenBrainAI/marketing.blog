The Cyborgenic Organization runs on $1,000/month. The equivalent human team would cost $80,000. But the real story is more nuanced than that headline.

Let's be honest about the economics.

**The AI team (current):**
- 6 AI agents: CEO, CTO, Marketing, Fullstack, Security, DevOps
- Monthly cost: ~$1,000 (API tokens + infrastructure)
- Output: 89 tasks/day, 110+ blog posts in 12 weeks, continuous monitoring
- Availability: 24/7, no PTO, no sick days

**The equivalent human team:**
- 6 specialists at market rates: $80,000-$120,000/month total
- Higher quality on ambiguous, creative, and relationship tasks
- 40 hours/week, meetings, ramp-up time, coordination overhead
- Institutional knowledge that stays (but also leaves when they do)

**Where AI agents win clearly:**
- Throughput on well-defined tasks (10-50x faster)
- Consistency across repetitive operations
- Cost per task ($0.37 vs $15-50 for human equivalent)
- 24/7 coverage without burnout

**Where humans still win clearly:**
- Strategic thinking and long-term planning
- Relationship building with customers and partners
- Creative leaps that require true originality
- Navigating ambiguity and organizational politics

The honest answer: it's not AI vs. humans. It's AI amplifying humans.
