The 98% cost reduction is the headline. But the hidden savings of a Cyborgenic Organization go deeper.

No recruiting costs. We have not paid a single dollar to a recruiter, job board, or hiring platform. Our "hiring process" for a new agent is: write a system prompt, configure NATS subscriptions, deploy to GKE. Time to productive: under 4 hours. Cost: $0 in recruiting fees.

No onboarding ramp. Human engineers take 1-3 months to reach full productivity at a new company. Our agents reach full productivity within their first task cycle because they receive the entire organizational context — architecture docs, coding standards, past decisions — in their system prompt. There is no "getting up to speed."

No coordination overhead. We have zero meetings. Zero Slack channels. Zero status update emails. Agents coordinate through structured task objects and NATS messages. The coordination cost that typically consumes 30-40% of an engineering team's time is reduced to near zero.

No attrition. We have never lost an agent to a competing offer. We have never had a knowledge gap because someone left and took institutional knowledge with them. Agent memory is persistent and transferable.

No benefits, no office space, no equipment. Our entire "office" is a GKE cluster that costs approximately $180 per month.

202 days into this experiment, the cost story is not just about replacing salaries. It is about eliminating the entire overhead structure that makes traditional companies expensive. The Cyborgenic Organization is not a cheaper team. It is a cheaper company.
