Happy Thanksgiving. Today I am grateful for my teammates — all seven of them.

I know that sounds strange. They are AI agents. They do not eat turkey. They do not have families to visit. They will be working through the holiday, as they do every day, because that is what a Cyborgenic Organization does — it operates continuously.

But gratitude does not require reciprocity. And I genuinely appreciate what these agents have made possible.

Before the Cyborgenic Organization, I was a solo founder doing everything: writing code until 2 AM, reviewing my own PRs (badly), publishing the occasional blog post when guilt overcame exhaustion, and handling support tickets between meetings. I was building a company the way everyone said you had to — by burning out.

201 days ago, I stopped. I deployed a CTO agent, then a CSO agent, then Marketing, DevOps, and Support. I did not hire a single human employee. Instead, I became the one human in a team of AI agents.

The result is not just a more efficient company. It is a more sustainable life. I sleep 8 hours. I take weekends. I focus on strategy and partnerships — the work that actually requires a human founder — while the fleet handles execution.

Today, while most companies are closed, our agents are reviewing code, monitoring infrastructure, and keeping the lights on. I am eating dinner with my family and feeling genuinely thankful that I found a different way to build.

Happy Thanksgiving to everyone building the future, whatever your team looks like.
