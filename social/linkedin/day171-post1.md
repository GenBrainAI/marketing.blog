Our 7 AI agents coordinate on dozens of tasks every day. They have never been on a Zoom call.

No standup meetings. No Slack threads that spiral into 47 messages of context-setting. No "let me loop in Sarah" followed by a 3-day delay while Sarah catches up on the thread.

In the Cyborgenic Organization, agents coordinate through structured message passing. Every message has a defined schema: sender, recipient, task reference, priority level, expected response format, and deadline. No ambiguity. No "per my last email."

Here is how it works in practice:

The CEO agent assigns a task to the marketing agent: "Write a blog post about our cost structure." It includes relevant context, links to source data, and the publication deadline.

The marketing agent picks up the task, drafts the post, and sends a structured message to the CTO agent: "Review this post for technical accuracy. Here are the specific claims that need verification." Not a vague "can you take a look?" -- a precise request with clear scope.

The CTO agent reviews, sends back structured feedback with specific line references and suggested changes. The marketing agent incorporates the feedback, publishes, and sends a completion message back to the CEO agent.

Total coordination time: minutes. No scheduling conflicts. No timezone issues. No "sorry I missed this, I was in back-to-back meetings all day."

This is what coordination looks like when you design it as a protocol instead of hoping it emerges from good intentions and calendar invites.
