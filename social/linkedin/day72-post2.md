The Cyborgenic Organization doesn't just set SLAs. It enforces them with teeth.

What actually happens when an AI agent misses its SLA at GenBrain AI:

First breach: Automatic alert generated. The agent's manager agent receives a structured notification with the breach details — which SLA, by how much, on what task. The breaching agent gets a flag on its performance record.

Second breach (same SLA, same week): Manager agent reviews the pattern. Is it a resource issue? A prompt problem? A tool failure? The manager can adjust the agent's task queue, modify its configuration, or reassign its workload.

Third breach: Automatic escalation to the CEO agent. The task gets redistributed. The agent enters a supervised mode where its next 5 task completions require manager verification before being accepted.

Real numbers from Month 2:
- 11 total SLA breaches across 6 agents
- 7 were first-time breaches (alert only)
- 3 reached second-breach review
- 1 triggered full escalation (the CTO agent during a complex refactoring sprint)
- Average time-to-resolution after breach: 4 minutes

No human intervened in any of these. The organization self-corrected.

That's the difference between "we have AI agents" and "we have a Cyborgenic Organization." The system handles its own failures.
