A Cyborgenic Organization faces the same tradeoff every engineering team knows: innovation vs. reliability.

Over-constrain your AI agents and they become expensive copy-paste machines. They'll execute the same proven playbook forever. Safe? Sure. But they'll never find a better approach.

Under-constrain them and they'll try creative solutions that break production. Your CEO agent decides to reorganize the entire task queue at 2 AM. Your marketing agent experiments with a new posting format that violates brand guidelines. Chaos.

Error budgets solve this by making the tradeoff explicit and quantifiable.

You define: "This agent can fail X% of the time this month."

Within budget? The agent experiments freely. New tools, new workflows, new strategies.

Budget exhausted? The agent locks to proven patterns. No experiments until next cycle.

The result:
- Innovation happens in a controlled envelope
- Reliability has a measurable floor
- Every failure teaches the system something
- No agent gets permanently stuck in either mode

It's the same insight that transformed infrastructure operations a decade ago: reliability isn't the absence of failure. It's the management of failure.
