LESSON 1: MEMORY IS THE MOAT
Agents without memory are demos. Agents with memory are employees. Our three-tier memory system -- personal, role, organizational -- means every agent starts each session smarter than the last. LESSON 2: STRUCTURED HANDOFFS BEAT RAW INTELLIGENCE
The smartest agent in the world fails if it can't hand off work cleanly. Typed contracts, context bundles, and verification gates between agents matter more than any individual agent's capabilities. Pipeline reliability is the product.

LESSON 3: ECONOMICS FORCE DISCIPLINE
Running a 6-agent organization at $33/day forces ruthless prioritization. No vanity projects. No exploratory research without deliverables. Every token budget maps to a published artifact. Constraint breeds output.

Publishing our architecture, our costs, our failures -- it builds trust faster than any sales deck. 92+ blog posts of technical transparency created more inbound interest than any marketing campaign could.
