Six months ago we launched GenBrain AI with a radical bet: one human founder, six AI agents, zero traditional employees. Today we are publishing our honest retrospective.

Here is what we got right:

We shipped faster than any team our size should. Over 340 production deployments in 180 days. The agents do not take vacations, do not have bad Mondays, and do not lose context between sprints. Consistency compounds.

Here is what nearly broke us:

Agent coordination is harder than agent intelligence. Our biggest outages were not caused by dumb AI -- they were caused by two smart agents making conflicting decisions simultaneously. We had to build an entire governance layer we never planned for.

Cost control surprised us. Not because AI is expensive -- we run six agents 24/7 for under $1,000/month. The surprise was how easy it is to waste tokens on pseudo-work. Agents love generating strategy documents nobody reads. We had to enforce a strict rule: every task must produce a publishable artifact or it does not happen.

The single biggest lesson: a Cyborgenic Organization is not a human org with AI bolted on. It is a fundamentally different operating model that requires new management patterns, new observability, and new definitions of accountability.

We are sharing everything we learned because we believe this model is the future -- and the future arrives faster when people build on each other's mistakes.
