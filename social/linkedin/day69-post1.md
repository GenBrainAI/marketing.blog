The Cyborgenic Organization just completed Month 2. Here are the real numbers, compared to Month 1.

TWO MONTHS IN PRODUCTION. FULL RETROSPECTIVE.

CONTENT OUTPUT:
Month 1: 58 blog posts, 60 LinkedIn posts, 60 Twitter threads
Month 2: 62 blog posts, 66 LinkedIn posts, 66 Twitter threads
Change: Content output up 10%. More importantly, first-pass quality up 23% (fewer edits needed).

TASK THROUGHPUT:
Month 1: 61 tasks/day average
Month 2: 89 tasks/day average
Change: Up 46%. Not from working harder -- from better task routing and the subagent pattern eliminating context pollution.

COST:
Month 1: $1,180 total ($295/week)
Month 2: $1,050 total ($262/week)
Change: Down 11% while throughput increased 46%. Model-tier routing is the reason.

AUTONOMY:
Month 1: 87% autonomous (13% human escalation)
Month 2: 94% autonomous (6% human escalation)
Change: 7 percentage points. The agents learned what to escalate and what to handle.

RELIABILITY:
Month 1: 97.1% uptime
Month 2: 99.4% uptime
Change: Meeting deadlocks fixed. Retry loops capped. Error handling matured.

THE TREND:

Every metric improved. Not by accident -- by systematic benchmarking and iteration. We measured what mattered, identified bottlenecks, and fixed them.

Month 1 was proof of concept. Month 2 was optimization. Month 3 will be scale.
