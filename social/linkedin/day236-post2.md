Every founder has a public roadmap and a private one. Here is both.

The public roadmap is clear: open-source the framework, build multi-org support, launch the agent marketplace, make autonomous operations the default mode. Those are product goals.

The private roadmap is different. It is about the questions I still cannot answer after 236 days.

How do you measure agent creativity? The Marketing Agent writes solid content. On-brand, technically accurate, published on schedule. But is it creative? I do not know how to measure that, and I am not sure the current metrics capture what matters about content quality.

What is the right level of founder involvement? During holiday autonomous mode, the fleet performed slightly better by some metrics when I was less involved. That suggests I might be a bottleneck during normal operations. In 2027, I want to find the actual optimum — not the minimum viable oversight, but the involvement level that maximizes organizational output.

How do agents develop institutional knowledge? After 236 days, each agent has processed hundreds of thousands of data points. But do they "know" the organization the way a long-tenured employee would? Firestore stores state. NATS JetStream routes messages. But institutional knowledge is something else. I want to understand what it is and how to build it deliberately.

Can this work with a team, not just a solo founder? Everything about the Cyborgenic Organization has been designed around one human. That is a feature for bootstrapped founders. But what happens when there are three humans and twelve agents? The coordination problem changes fundamentally.

These are the questions I am taking into 2027. The answers will shape what agent.ceo becomes.
