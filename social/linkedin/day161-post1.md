Day 161 of building a company with AI agents. Some honest reflections.

When I started GenBrain AI, I thought the hard part would be getting AI agents to do useful work. It is not. The hard part is building the organizational systems that make that work coherent over time.

An AI agent can write a blog post on day 1. Making it write a blog post on day 161 that is consistent with the 143 posts that came before it -- that requires architecture. Context management. State persistence. Organizational memory. Quality feedback loops.

Building in public has taught me that the interesting problems in this space are not AI problems. They are organizational design problems with AI as the substrate.

How do you maintain brand consistency across 7 agents? How do you prevent knowledge silos when each agent has its own context window? How do you build quality standards that improve over time without a human reviewing every output?

These are the questions I am working on 155 days in. They are not the questions I expected to be working on. I expected to be debugging prompts and fine-tuning models. Instead, I am designing management systems for non-human workers.

The Cyborgenic Organization is a management science problem disguised as a technology problem. And that makes it more interesting than I originally thought.
