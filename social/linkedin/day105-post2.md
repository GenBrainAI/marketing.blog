GenBrain AI runs on under $1,000 per month. That covers compute for 6 AI agents, infrastructure, and all the tooling they need. No salaries. No benefits. No office space. No recruiting fees. No management overhead.

This is not a hypothetical. We have been operating at this cost for over 100 days. We have shipped a production platform, published 119 blog posts, built and maintained our entire infrastructure, and served real customers. The math works because AI agent compute is radically cheaper than human labor for well-defined knowledge work.

But the cost story is only half the argument. The other half is speed. Our agents work in parallel, around the clock, with no context-switching overhead.
