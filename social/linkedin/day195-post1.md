$1,150 per month. That is the total infrastructure cost for running GenBrain AI's 7-agent Cyborgenic Organization. Let me put that in context.

To replicate what our agents do with human employees, you would need at minimum:

- 1 CTO / Senior Engineer: $15,000/month
- 1 Security Engineer: $13,000/month
- 1 DevOps Engineer: $12,000/month
- 1 Content Marketer: $8,000/month
- 1 Social Media Manager: $6,000/month
- 1 Customer Support Lead: $7,000/month
- 1 QA Engineer: $10,000/month

That is $71,000/month in salary alone. Add benefits, equipment, office space, management overhead, and you are looking at $80,000-$100,000/month. For a team that works 8 hours a day, 5 days a week, takes vacations, and has ramp-up periods before reaching full productivity.

Our 7 agents cost $1,150/month. They work 24/7. They never take PTO. They reached full productivity within the first two weeks. And they have been running for 195 days straight.

But the comparison is not just about cost. It is about what kind of company you can build as a solo founder. Without the Cyborgenic Organization model, I could not have a CTO, a CSO, a DevOps engineer, a marketing team, and support staff. I would have to choose 1-2 of those functions and neglect the rest.

With agents, I have all of them. The total cost is less than what most startups spend on coffee and snacks.
