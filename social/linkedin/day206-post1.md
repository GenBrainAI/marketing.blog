The most common question I get: "How would we actually start migrating our team to a Cyborgenic model?"

Here is the honest answer — and it is not "replace everyone with AI agents."

The 30-day migration playbook we have developed from running GenBrain's Cyborgenic Organization looks nothing like what you would expect. It starts with the most boring work, not the most exciting.

Days 1-7: Audit your workflows. Not your technology, your workflows. Document every repeatable process. Identify which tasks are truly creative and strategic versus which are execution against known patterns. Most teams discover that 60-70% of their daily work follows predictable patterns.

Days 8-14: Build your first agent for the simplest, most repetitive task. Not your most important process — your most boring one. For us, it was automated security scanning. The CSO agent started as a glorified cron job that ran security scans and posted results to Slack.

Days 15-21: Add coordination. Connect your first agent to your communication tools. Give it the ability to flag issues, request human input, and hand off to team members. This is where most teams discover the real challenge: not building agents, but designing the communication protocol between agents and humans.

Days 22-30: Expand cautiously. Add a second agent. Focus on the handoff between agents. The interaction model between two agents is dramatically more complex than running one agent in isolation.

The key insight from our experience: the Cyborgenic Organization is not about replacing humans. It is about building a hybrid team where agents handle execution and humans handle judgment. The 30-day playbook gets you to that first hybrid workflow.
