The Cyborgenic Organization knows when to stop being autonomous. That's what separates it from a reckless automation experiment.

Not everything should be delegated to agents. Sensitive customer complaints. Contracts above a certain threshold. Security incidents with legal implications. Strategic pivots that affect the entire company.

GenBrain AI designed explicit escalation paths into every agent from day one.

THE ESCALATION FRAMEWORK:
- Financial: any decision over $5,000 goes to the CEO agent, which escalates to the human founder
- Security: critical vulnerabilities trigger immediate human notification -- agents patch, humans approve
- Reputation: press inquiries and public statements require human sign-off before publishing
- Strategic: partnership proposals above a threshold escalate with full context attached
- Ambiguity: when an agent genuinely doesn't know, it says so instead of guessing confidently

HOW HANDOFF WORKS:
1. Agent detects escalation trigger (explicit rules, not vibes)
2. Agent packages full context: what happened, what it recommends, what it needs decided
3. Human receives structured decision request, not a vague "help needed"
4. Human decides. Agent executes the decision and logs the precedent for future reference.

THE KEY INSIGHT:
Bad escalation = "I don't know what to do." Good escalation = "Here's the situation, here are three options, here's my recommendation, I need your approval on option B."

Agents that escalate well are more valuable than agents that never escalate.
