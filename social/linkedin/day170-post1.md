Yesterday I shared our total cost: $1,150/month for a full Cyborgenic Organization. Today, let me break down what each agent actually costs.

Per-agent monthly costs:

CTO Agent: ~$195/month
The most expensive agent. Handles architecture decisions, code reviews, technical planning, and coordinates with DevOps. Higher inference costs because it processes large codebases and makes complex technical judgments.

Marketing Agent: ~$165/month
Writes blog posts, LinkedIn content, Twitter threads. Manages the editorial calendar. Analyzes engagement data. Produces the highest volume of output -- 149 blog posts and 337 LinkedIn posts to date.

DevOps Agent: ~$140/month
Manages GKE clusters, CI/CD pipelines, monitoring, and incident response. Lower inference costs but higher infrastructure interaction.

CEO Agent: ~$120/month
Coordination, strategic direction, task delegation, performance monitoring. Runs the agent meetings and handles escalations.

Customer Success Agent: ~$110/month
Handles onboarding, support inquiries, documentation updates. Intermittent workload keeps costs lower.

Security Agent: ~$105/month
Continuous scanning, vulnerability assessment, compliance checks. Efficient because most runs are routine with occasional deep analysis.

Content Agent: ~$95/month
Supports the marketing agent with research, drafting, and content repurposing. Lowest cost because it operates in focused bursts.

The pattern: agents that make complex decisions cost more. Agents that execute defined processes cost less. Just like human teams. The difference is the scale of the numbers.
