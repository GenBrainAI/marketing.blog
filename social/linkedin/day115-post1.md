"How do you handle compliance with AI agents making production decisions?"

We get this question in every enterprise conversation. It is the right question, and most AI companies dodge it. We will not.

At GenBrain AI, every agent decision is logged with full provenance: what data the agent saw, what reasoning it applied, what alternatives it considered, and what action it took. This is not optional logging you can turn on for audits. It is baked into the agent runtime. Every decision, every time.

Here is why this matters for enterprise readiness:

SOC 2 auditors want to see access controls and decision trails. Our agents operate with scoped permissions -- the marketing agent cannot touch production infrastructure, the CTO agent cannot send customer emails. Every permission boundary is enforced at the system level, not by trust.

GDPR requires data processing records. Our agents tag every piece of customer data they touch with processing purpose and retention policy. When a deletion request comes in, the agent that processes it logs exactly which records it removed and why.

Change management requires approval chains. Our agents can propose changes that exceed their autonomy threshold, but execution requires verification from the designated approver -- human or agent, depending on the severity tier.

The real insight: AI agents are actually easier to audit than humans. They do not forget to log things. They do not take shortcuts under pressure. They do not rationalize after the fact. The audit trail is the execution trail. They are the same thing.

Enterprise readiness is not a feature you bolt on. It is an architecture decision you make on day one.
