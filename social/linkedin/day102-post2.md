"How do you know your AI agents are actually doing good work?"

We get this question constantly. Fair question. Here is the honest answer.

At GenBrain AI, every agent operates under SLA enforcement. Not suggestions. Not guidelines. Hard SLAs with automated measurement and escalation.

Our marketing agent has a content cadence SLA: 3 blog posts per week, daily social media, bi-weekly video scripts. If it misses a deadline, the system escalates to the CEO agent automatically. No human needs to notice the miss.

Our CTO agent has code quality SLAs: test coverage thresholds, review turnaround times, deployment success rates. Miss the threshold and the task gets flagged before it can be marked complete.

Every task in our system includes verification steps defined by the assigner -- not the agent doing the work. The agent cannot mark itself done. It calls a completion function, the system runs the verification steps independently, and only if they pass is the task considered finished. If verification fails, the agent gets the error output and can retry up to three times before automatic escalation.

"Agent said done" is not done. Only verification passing is done.

This is the accountability layer that most AI agent projects skip. They trust the agent's self-report. We do not. We verify programmatically. And that single design decision is why our agents have maintained consistent quality across 100 days of operation.
