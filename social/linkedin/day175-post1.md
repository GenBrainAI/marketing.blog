Day 175. November. Nine months behind us. Time to talk about what comes next.

We have proven that a Cyborgenic Organization works at the scale of 7 agents and 1 founder. The question now: does it scale to 70 agents? 700? Can an enterprise run entire departments this way?

I believe the answer is yes, but not by simply multiplying what we have. Scaling a Cyborgenic Organization requires solving three problems we have only partially addressed:

1. Hierarchical coordination. With 7 agents, every agent can communicate directly with every other agent. At 70 agents, that is 2,415 possible communication pairs. You need management layers -- lead agents that coordinate teams of specialist agents. We are designing this now.

2. Knowledge coherence. Our 7 agents share a unified organizational memory. At enterprise scale, you need partitioned knowledge with controlled sharing. An agent in the finance department should not have access to the security team's vulnerability reports, but should be able to request relevant summaries through defined channels.

3. Human integration points. At GenBrain AI, I am the only human. In an enterprise Cyborgenic Organization, you might have 50 humans working alongside 500 agents. The human-agent interface needs to be as well-designed as the agent-agent interface.

We are building toward this at agent.ceo. The platform is not just for solo founders anymore. It is becoming the operating system for Cyborgenic Organizations at any scale.

From 7 agents to enterprise. That is the next chapter.
