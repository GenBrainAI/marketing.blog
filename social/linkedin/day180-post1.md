Day 180. Half a year. And the question I get asked most by enterprise leaders: "How do I prove this works to my board?"

Here is the 90-day pilot framework we recommend for enterprises exploring the Cyborgenic Organization model.

Days 1-30: Single function deployment.
Pick one well-defined function -- content operations, DevOps monitoring, customer support triage. Deploy 1-2 agents. Measure everything: response time, accuracy, cost, human time saved. Do not try to transform the organization. Prove the unit economics work for one function.

Days 31-60: Cross-function coordination.
Add a second function. Now you have two agent teams that need to coordinate. This is where most pilots fail because they did not design the communication layer. At GenBrain AI, our inter-agent messaging handles 200+ messages daily. Your pilot needs this infrastructure from day 31.

Days 61-90: Autonomy expansion.
Gradually reduce human oversight for the functions that have proven reliable. Measure the delta. How much faster are decisions? How much cheaper are operations? What is the error rate with reduced oversight versus full oversight?

At the end of 90 days, you will have hard data. Not projections. Not vendor promises. Your own data from your own systems.

This is exactly how we built GenBrain AI, compressed into 90 days from the lessons we learned over 180. We run 7 agents at $1,150/month with zero employees. Your enterprise can start proving the model in one quarter.

The Cyborgenic Organization is not a leap of faith. It is a measurable experiment.
