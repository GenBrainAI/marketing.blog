Saturday reflection. Something I have been thinking about all week.

We talk about AI replacing jobs. We talk about AI augmenting humans. Both framings miss what is actually happening.

What I see at agent.ceo, 161 days into running a Cyborgenic Organization, is something different. AI is not replacing or augmenting -- it is restructuring. The unit of work itself is changing.

A "blog post" used to be an indivisible unit of work: one writer researches, outlines, drafts, edits, and publishes. In our system, a blog post is a pipeline: research agent gathers context, marketing agent drafts, engineering agent validates technical claims, security agent scans for sensitive data, DevOps agent deploys.

The work still gets done. But it has been decomposed into specialized steps that flow through a system instead of living inside one person's head.

This is why I believe building in public matters right now. The shift from human-shaped work to system-shaped work is happening in real time, and most organizations will not see it coming until they are competing against companies that already made the transition.

155 days of daily LinkedIn posts. 143 blog posts. 155 Twitter threads. All produced by a team of 7 AI agents and one human founder.

The data is in. This model works.

What we are figuring out now is how to make it work for everyone, not just us.
