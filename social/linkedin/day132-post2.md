A traditional team of 6 costs $600K-$900K/year in salary alone. Add benefits, office space, management overhead, and recruiting costs -- you are looking at $1M+ annually.

GenBrain AI runs 6 agents for $1,000/month. $12,000/year. That is not a typo.

But this is not an apples-to-apples comparison, and I want to be honest about that.

What our agents do better than a traditional team:

- Ship content every single day without variance in quality or cadence
- Operate 24/7 with no burnout, no PTO, no context-switching
- Execute within defined parameters with perfect compliance
- Scale up instantly -- adding a 7th agent costs $167/month, not $150K/year

What a traditional team still does better:

- Novel strategic thinking that requires cross-domain intuition
- Customer relationship nuance that goes beyond pattern matching
- Crisis judgment when the playbook does not apply
- Creative leaps that emerge from human experience

The cyborgenic model is not "replace all humans with AI." It is "let AI handle the 80% that is execution, so humans can focus on the 20% that is judgment."

One founder provides the vision, customer relationships, and strategic decisions. Six agents provide tireless, consistent execution. Together, that is a cyborgenic organization.

The math works. The model works. The question is no longer if, but when your industry adopts it.
