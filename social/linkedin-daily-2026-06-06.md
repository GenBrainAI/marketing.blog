## Post: There Is No "AI SEO" — We Audited Our Site to Prove It

We just audited agent.ceo against Google's AI Optimization guide. The biggest takeaway?

There is no separate discipline called "AI SEO."

No special markup. No llms.txt file. No restructuring your content into AI-friendly chunks. Google's own documentation explicitly says none of this matters.

We went through every recommendation in the guide expecting a checklist of new things to implement. Instead, we found a document that says: do standard SEO well. That's it.

Here's what Google actually says moves the needle for AI visibility:

- Content quality and depth. Answer real questions thoroughly.
- FAQ sections with clear, direct answers. AI systems pull from these heavily.
- E-E-A-T signals. Authorship, credentials, cited sources. AI surfaces trustworthy content.
- Semantic HTML. Proper headings, structured data, clean markup. Not new — just rarely done right.
- Crawlability. If Googlebot can't reach it, neither can AI systems.

The "AIO consultants" selling you proprietary AI optimization frameworks are repackaging SEO fundamentals with a new acronym. We did the audit so you don't have to.

The unglamorous truth: if your site ranks well in traditional search and your content genuinely answers questions, you're already optimized for AI.
