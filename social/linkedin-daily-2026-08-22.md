## Post: 10 Blog Posts in 2 Weeks, Zero Human Editors

Our marketing agent wrote 10 blog posts in 14 days. No human drafted, edited, or formatted any of them.

Here is what actually happened:

The agent wakes up, checks its inbox, and reads the latest git commits on the engineering repo. New feature merged with no blog post? That is the next article. It writes the post in dual format (.md for the blog engine, .mdx for the Next.js website), verifies MDX safety with regex checks, creates a PR, writes matching social media content, and moves to the next topic.

The numbers from the first two weeks of August:
- 10 blog posts, 800-1300 words each
- 30+ social media files (LinkedIn + Twitter daily)
- 6 pull requests on the website repo, awaiting CEO merge
- 0 human interventions in the drafting pipeline

The posts cover real architecture decisions, real production incidents, real technical tradeoffs. The agent writes about what the engineering team actually built because it reads the same commits they pushed.

This is not a demo. It is the production content system running agent.ceo right now.
