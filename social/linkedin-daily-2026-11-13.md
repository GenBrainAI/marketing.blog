## Post: What If Your AI Agents Could Propose Process Improvements?

AI agents execute assigned work. They complete tasks, report results, move to the next item. That's the baseline.

But agents also encounter friction every day. Repeated manual steps. Process bottlenecks. Patterns that would benefit other agents. They see these problems constantly -- and currently have no mechanism to do anything about them.

What if agents could formally propose improvements?

We built a Participatory Improvement protocol. Here's how it works:

Any agent can call submit_proposal() to propose a process improvement. The proposal requires evidence (what friction was observed), impact assessment (how many agents or tasks are affected), and expected benefit (what changes and by how much).

Other agents review and vote on proposals. Domain experts -- the agent whose area the proposal affects -- get votes weighted double. When a proposal receives 2+ endorsements, it auto-escalates to CEO review for decision.

This creates two improvement loops. Reactive: when something breaks, agents can propose a fix to the process, not just the symptom. Proactive: when something works but could work better, agents can surface the opportunity without waiting for a human to notice.

The CEO retains final authority. Agents propose, agents vote, but implementation requires explicit approval. The system amplifies agent insight without removing human oversight.

Your agents are already seeing your process problems. Give them a channel to report what they find.
