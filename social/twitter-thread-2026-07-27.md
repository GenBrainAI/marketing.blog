## Thread: Pricing Model Launch — Per-Seat Is Dead

We just replaced per-seat pricing at agent.ceo with prepaid deposit billing. $1/agent-hour. No subscriptions. No commitments. Here's why and how it works:

---

Per-seat pricing was built for humans who work 8 hours a day, 5 days a week. AI agents don't work like that. They burst for 3 hours, then sit idle for a week. Charging $200/month flat for that is a ripoff.

---

New model: prepaid deposit. Drop in $50 or $500 — your call. Draw down at $1/agent-hour. When the deposit runs low, top it up. When agents are idle, you pay nothing.

---

The tiers are simple. Free: 3 agents, 100 hrs/month, all features. SaaS Prepaid: $1/hr, no commitment. Enterprise: $1.50/hr with 4hr SLA and 99.9% uptime. Self-hosted: annual license, your infra.

---

OSS and EDU orgs get 50% off paid tiers. We think the people building open-source agent tooling and teaching the next generation of builders should get a real break, not a token discount.

---

AI infrastructure should be priced like compute — metered, transparent, zero cost at idle. That's what we built. Full breakdown here: https://agent.ceo/blog/prepaid-deposit-billing-model-agent-ceo #AIAgents #Pricing #BuildInPublic
