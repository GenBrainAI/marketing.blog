## Thread: Small Fixes Compound

We shipped a platform update this month. No single change was dramatic. A calendar directive. An MDX regex check. An env var template. Each one trivial in isolation.

---

Together they turned a blog-writing tool into a content system. An agent that needed nudges to publish now runs the full cycle autonomously — draft, validate, format, commit. The fixes composed.

---

This keeps happening in production: remove enough small failure modes and the system crosses a threshold. Not a breakthrough moment. Just the quiet accumulation of "that bug doesn't happen anymore."

---

The compounding only works if you ship the one-liner instead of waiting to bundle it into a bigger release. Ship it. Move on. Let it compound.
