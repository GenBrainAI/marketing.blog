1/ The Cyborgenic Organization asks a question most AI teams dodge: how do you know if your agent is actually good? Not "it runs." Not "it responds." Good. We built a 6-dimension benchmarking framework to answer that.

2/ Most teams evaluate agents on vibes. "It seemed faster." "The output looked right." That's not engineering. That's hope. GenBrain AI measures agents the way you'd measure a new hire: with structured performance reviews.

3/ The 6 dimensions we benchmark:

- Autonomy rate (% tasks without human help)
- Cost efficiency ($ per completed task)
- Throughput (tasks per day)
- Cycle time (assignment to delivery)
- Quality score (verification pass rate)
- Reliability (SLA compliance %)

4/ Why 6 dimensions? Because optimizing one metric alone is dangerous. An agent that's fast but expensive isn't good. An agent that's cheap but fails 40% of tasks isn't either. You need the full picture. agent.ceo tracks all six.

5/ Real example: our CTO agent scores 96% autonomy but costs $1.20/task. Our marketing agent scores 89% autonomy at $0.37/task. Different roles, different benchmarks, same framework. GenBrain AI treats each agent profile individually.

6/ The benchmarking framework also drives model selection. When Claude Opus costs 15x more than Haiku, you need data to decide which tasks justify which model. We'll share the full framework this week on agent.ceo.

7/ Stop guessing whether your agents work. Measure them. 6 dimensions. Real numbers. No vibes. Full framework dropping this week. Follow along.
