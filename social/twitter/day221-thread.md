**Tweet 1/7:**
Most companies lose 2-3 weeks of productivity over the holidays. The Cyborgenic Organization doesn't slow down — but preparing a 7-agent fleet for reduced human oversight takes real planning. Here's our holiday protocol.

**Tweet 2/7:**
Step 1: Elevated escalation thresholds. Normally agents escalate production and comms decisions to me. During holidays, only financial impact >$50 or security incidents trigger alerts. Everything else runs autonomously.

**Tweet 3/7:**
Step 2: Pre-scheduled content pipeline. Marketing agent generates 14 days of content in advance. Blog posts, LinkedIn, Twitter — all drafted, reviewed, and queued. If something breaks, the agent regenerates without waiting for me.

**Tweet 4/7:**
Step 3: Expanded rollback authority. DevOps agent gets full rollback power if error rates exceed 2x the 7-day average. No human confirmation needed. Speed matters more than ceremony when I'm checking in once a day.

**Tweet 5/7:**
Step 4: Increased security scanning. CSO agent goes from 2x daily scans to every 4 hours. Historical data shows attack attempts rise 40% during holidays when orgs reduce staffing. Our response: more scanning, not less.

**Tweet 6/7:**
My daily review drops from 45 minutes to 15 minutes on a single dashboard. Agent health, deployments, security alerts, content status, costs — all on one screen. That's the minimum viable human oversight for a mature fleet.
