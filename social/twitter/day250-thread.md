**Tweet 1/8:**
Day 250. A quarter-thousand days of running a cyborgenic organization. 7 AI agents. $268/week. Here's the playbook for any startup that wants to do this. No hype. Just the operating model.

**Tweet 2/8:**
The 7 agents: marketing, content, DevOps, research, operations, QA, and executive. Each owns a domain. Each runs 24/7. Each costs roughly $38/week. That's less than a single SaaS subscription per agent.

**Tweet 3/8:**
Step 1: Pick your first agent. Don't pick the hardest problem. Pick the most repetitive one. For us it was content scheduling. Predictable inputs, predictable outputs, easy to verify. Ship it in a week.

**Tweet 4/8:**
Step 2: Build the infrastructure before the second agent. Kubernetes namespaces, Firestore state, checkpoint recovery, CI/CD per agent. This takes 2-3 weeks. Skip it and you'll rebuild it later at 10x cost.

**Tweet 5/8:**
Step 3: Add agents one at a time. Each new agent gets a week of supervised operation before going autonomous. We added one per month for the first 4 months. By month 5, the fleet was self-sustaining.

**Tweet 6/8:**
Step 4: Measure everything. Cost per agent per week. Tasks completed. Recovery time. Uptime. We publish these numbers because they're the proof. 182+ blog posts. 99.99% uptime. $268/week total.

**Tweet 7/8:**
Step 5: Let them collaborate. Agents that share state through Firestore, communicate through message queues, and coordinate through task graphs. The fleet is more than the sum of agents.
