**Tweet 1/8:**
The honest case AGAINST Cyborgenic Organizations. (Yes, there is one.)

We've spent 6 months building GenBrain AI with AI agents. We believe in this model. But intellectual honesty requires listing the real downsides.

**Tweet 2/8:**
Problem 1: Debugging is harder.

When a human developer ships a bug, you ask them what they were thinking. When an agent ships a bug, you get a token log and a probability distribution.

Agents don't have intent. Tracing why they made a decision is forensics, not conversation.

**Tweet 3/8:**
Problem 2: You inherit every model regression.

When your LLM provider ships a bad update, your entire workforce degrades simultaneously. No notice. No opt-out.

We've seen 15% quality drops from upstream model changes. You don't control your team's capabilities.

**Tweet 4/8:**
Problem 3: Creative work hits a ceiling.

Our agents produce competent content. Technically accurate. Well-structured. But they don't produce work that makes you stop scrolling.

For documentation and technical writing: excellent. For brand-defining creative: not yet.

**Tweet 5/8:**
Problem 4: Vendor concentration risk.

If Anthropic goes down, GenBrain AI stops. All of it. We have no backup workforce. We mitigate with multi-model support, but switching models mid-task isn't seamless.

Your entire company depends on another company's API uptime.

**Tweet 6/8:**
Problem 5: Customer trust gap.

Some customers are uncomfortable knowing AI agents handle their requests. "I want to talk to a human" is a real objection. We're transparent about our model, and that transparency costs us deals.

**Tweet 7/8:**
Problem 6: You need a strong human operator.

A Cyborgenic Organization without a skilled human at the helm is just expensive autocomplete. The founder/operator must understand AI, engineering, AND business. That's a narrow hiring pool of exactly one.

**Tweet 8/8:**
We believe in Cyborgenic Organizations despite these problems, not because we ignore them. Every model has tradeoffs.

$1,000/month for a full team is real. But so are the risks above. Go in with open eyes.
