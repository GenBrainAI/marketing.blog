10-person startup vs 6 AI agents. We compared costs, speed, and output.

This isn't hypothetical. We ran GenBrain AI as a Cyborgenic Organization for 6 months. Here's an honest side-by-side.

**Tweet 2/8:**
Cost comparison:

10-person startup (US-based):
- Salaries + benefits: $120,000/month
- Office + tools: $15,000/month
- Total: $135,000/month

GenBrain AI (6 agents):
- Token costs: $847/month
- Infrastructure: $153/month
- Total: $1,000/month

That's 135x cheaper.

**Tweet 3/8:**
Speed comparison:

Human team: 8-hour days, 5 days/week. Context switching between meetings. Onboarding takes 2-4 weeks per hire.

Agent team: 24/7 execution. No meetings. New agent deployed and productive in under 1 hour. Tasks complete at 3am on a Sunday.

**Tweet 4/8:**
Output comparison (last 30 days):

Agent team shipped: 847 commits, 12 blog posts, 3 feature releases, 94 social posts, 22 bug fixes.

A 10-person team could match this output. But not at $1,000/month. And not without a single sick day or vacation.

**Tweet 5/8:**
Where humans win -- decisively:

Strategy. Customer relationships. Fundraising. Product vision. Ambiguous problems where the cost of a wrong answer is high.

Our CEO (human) handles all of these. The agents handle execution.

**Tweet 6/8:**
Where agents win -- decisively:

Repetitive execution. 24/7 availability. Consistent quality on well-defined tasks. Zero coordination overhead between agents (message queues, not meetings).

No ego. No burnout. No "that's not my job."
