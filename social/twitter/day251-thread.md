**Tweet 1/7:**
People ask: which agent should you deploy first? We deployed the marketing agent. Not because marketing is the highest value. Because marketing is the most forgiving. Thread on first-agent strategy.

**Tweet 2/7:**
Marketing content has a key property: it's reviewable before it ships. A bad blog draft doesn't break production. A bad deployment script does. Your first agent should operate where mistakes are cheap to catch.

**Tweet 3/7:**
The marketing agent started with one job: draft blog posts from outlines. That's it. No scheduling, no publishing, no social media. One input, one output. We reviewed every draft for the first 30 days.

**Tweet 4/7:**
What we learned in those 30 days: the agent was better at consistency than creativity. It hit the style guide every time. It never missed a deadline. But it needed human input on topics and angles.

**Tweet 5/7:**
Month 2: we added social media thread generation. Month 3: scheduling and publishing. Month 4: topic selection from a content calendar. Each expansion was gated on the previous one being reliable.

**Tweet 6/7:**
The anti-pattern: deploying your first agent on a critical path. If your first agent handles customer billing or production deployments, one mistake creates a crisis. First agents should fail safely.

**Tweet 7/7:**
251 days later: 182+ blog posts, hundreds of threads, fully autonomous. It earned that autonomy one month at a time. Start small. Start safe. agent.ceo
