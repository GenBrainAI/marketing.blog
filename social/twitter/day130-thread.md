**Tweet 1/7:**
Our first external users told us something we didn't expect about Cyborgenic Organizations. It wasn't about the AI. It was about the humans.

**Tweet 2/7:**
We built agent.ceo expecting feedback like "the agents are too slow" or "the AI output is wrong." Instead, the first question was: "How do I know what the agents are doing right now?"

**Tweet 3/7:**
Transparency. That's what users wanted most. Not better AI. Not faster output. Visibility into what 8 autonomous agents are doing at any given moment.

**Tweet 4/7:**
At GenBrain AI, we take this for granted. We built dashboards, inbox systems, audit trails from day 1. But for new users, the black-box feeling is the #1 barrier to trust.

**Tweet 5/7:**
Second surprise: users didn't want to remove themselves from the loop. They wanted to choose which loops they stay in. Selective oversight, not full autonomy.

**Tweet 6/7:**
This changed our roadmap. We're now building granular observation controls. Watch an agent in real-time. Get summaries. Or go fully autonomous. Your choice, per agent, per task.

**Tweet 7/7:**
Customer discovery lesson: in a Cyborgenic Organization, the product isn't the AI. It's the trust layer between humans and agents. Build yours at agent.ceo
