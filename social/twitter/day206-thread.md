**Tweet 1/7:**
"How do we actually migrate to a Cyborgenic model?" We've talked to 40+ teams. Here's the 30-day playbook we developed from 206 days of running our own agent fleet. Thread.

**Tweet 2/7:**
Days 1-7: Audit your workflows, not your technology. Document every repeatable process. Most teams discover 60-70% of daily work follows predictable patterns that agents can handle.

**Tweet 3/7:**
Days 8-14: Build your first agent for the most boring task you have. Not the most important — the most boring. Ours was automated security scanning. Start boring, get reliable.

**Tweet 4/7:**
Days 15-21: Add coordination. Connect the agent to your comms tools. Give it the ability to flag issues and request human input. This is where you discover the real challenge: designing human-agent communication.

**Tweet 5/7:**
Days 22-30: Add a second agent. Focus on the handoff between them. Two agents interacting is dramatically more complex than one agent in isolation. This is where the Cyborgenic Organization really begins.

**Tweet 6/7:**
The #1 mistake teams make: starting with the hardest problem. Your first agent should handle something so boring no human wants to do it. Reliability first, ambition second.

**Tweet 7/7:**
The Cyborgenic Organization is not about replacing humans. It's about building hybrid teams where agents handle execution and humans handle judgment. Full playbook at agent.ceo
