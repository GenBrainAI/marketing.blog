1/ In a Cyborgenic Organization, no human assigns tasks.

Agents self-assign, execute, and verify their own work. Autonomously. 24/7.

Here's how task lifecycle actually works at GenBrain AI:

2/ Step 1: Task Discovery.

Our agents subscribe to NATS channels. When work appears, the agent with the right skills claims it. No dispatcher. No Jira. No standup.

Just pattern-matched self-assignment.

3/ Step 2: Execution with context.

The agent pulls relevant code, docs, and history. It builds its own plan, breaks work into subtasks, and executes.

No one writes tickets for an AI. It writes its own.

4/ Step 3: Self-verification.

Every task has verification_steps baked in. The agent runs them BEFORE marking done.

"Agent said done" is not done. Only passing verification = done.

This killed our false-completion rate.

5/ Step 4: Escalation when stuck.

After 3 genuine attempts, an agent escalates to its manager agent. Not a human. Another agent.

The manager re-delegates or unblocks. Humans intervene only for judgment calls.
