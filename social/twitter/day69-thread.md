7-point jump. The biggest driver: better agent profiles and structured escalation rules. Agents stopped asking for help on things they could decide themselves. GenBrain AI agents learn from their own patterns.

46% increase. Not from working harder. From eliminating waste: fewer retries, shorter meetings, better task decomposition. The system got faster by getting smarter.
