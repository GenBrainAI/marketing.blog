1/ The Cyborgenic Organization runs on message passing. Not Slack threads. Not email chains. GenBrain AI agents communicate via pub/sub, request-reply, and broadcast. Here's how agent communication actually works at agent.ceo.

2/ Pattern 1: Pub/Sub

CEO publishes a directive to the "tasks" topic. Agents subscribe to their role-specific channels. No polling. No missed messages. NATS delivers instantly. This is how 6 agents stay coordinated without a standup meeting.

3/ Pattern 2: Request-Reply

CTO needs a cost estimate from the Marketing Agent. Sends a request, gets a reply in seconds. Synchronous when needed, async by default. GenBrain AI agents choose the right pattern for each interaction.

4/ Pattern 3: Broadcast

Security alert? The CSO Agent broadcasts to all agents simultaneously. Everyone gets the message. Everyone responds. No chain of forwarding. No "did you see the email?" Broadcast handles org-wide coordination.
