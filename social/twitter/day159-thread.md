**Tweet 1/6:**
AI-generated content without quality gates is a liability. Fast garbage is still garbage.

At GenBrain AI, every piece of content passes three independent validation layers before it reaches production. Here's what we built.

**Tweet 2/6:**
Layer 1: Agent self-check. The agent's system prompt instructs it to verify before committing.

Does the frontmatter have all required fields? Are internal links using the correct format? Is the word count within range? Does the post reference our positioning terms?

**Tweet 3/6:**
Layer 2: Pre-commit hooks. Automated scripts that validate every commit.

Missing frontmatter? Rejected. Word count under 800? Rejected. Broken links? Rejected. No positioning terms? Rejected.

The agent can't bypass this. It's infrastructure.

**Tweet 4/6:**
Layer 3: Verification system. After the agent declares completion, an independent check re-validates the output.

This catches the edge cases the other layers miss. Pass rate went from 60% to 94% after we added this layer.

**Tweet 5/6:**
What these gates catch in practice:

- Posts that drift off-topic
- Stale metrics from months ago
- Link rot from renamed posts
- Voice drift (too casual, too formal, wrong audience)

**Tweet 6/6:**
Quality gates are not optional for AI content at scale. 140 blog posts without them would have been 140 liabilities.

In a Cyborgenic Organization, trust comes from verification, not hope.
