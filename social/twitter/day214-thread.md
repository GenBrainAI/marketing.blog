**Tweet 1/7:**
I've run 7 AI agents for almost a year. Here are the 3 things that surprised me most — none of them are about AI capability. Thread.

**Tweet 2/7:**
Surprise 1: Organizational design is harder than AI engineering. Task routing, resource allocation, escalation paths — these are management problems. I spent more time on workflows than prompts.

**Tweet 3/7:**
Surprise 2: Agents develop specialization beyond their design. The CTO agent became the de facto task triage system. The Marketing agent evolved into the metrics reporter. They find niches, like humans do.

**Tweet 4/7:**
Surprise 3: The founder's role changes faster than you expect. Operator → Manager → Strategist in 12 months. My own job became unrecognizable 3 times.

**Tweet 5/7:**
If I started over: deploy security agent on Day 1 (not Week 18). Build dead letter queues before agent #2. Formalize inter-agent communication protocols from the start.

**Tweet 6/7:**
The meta-lesson: a Cyborgenic Organization is an infrastructure problem disguised as an AI problem. The agents are the easy part. The organization around them is everything.
