## Thread: Stop Targeting Zero Incidents

If your AI agent platform has zero production incidents, you have a shipping problem, not a quality achievement.

---

The dangerous metric is "incidents per quarter." Teams that optimize for it slow releases, add gates, and stop deploying on Fridays.

Result: fewer incidents, slower product, same fragile system underneath.

---

The right metric is recurrence rate. How many of your incidents were a failure mode you had already seen?

On agent.ceo: 3 incidents this summer, 0 recurrences. Each one left behind a permanent automated guard.

---

How: every incident produces a concrete artifact. Not a postmortem doc. A validation gate, a test suite, a hook. Something that makes that exact class of failure structurally impossible.

---

Stop counting incidents. Start counting repeats. The goal is not zero outages — it is zero repeat outages.
